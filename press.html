About CalculatePilot

CalculatePilot is a free online calculator platform with more than 150 tools across finance, health, mathematics and everyday use. It runs in the browser and is also available as a Chrome extension, a Firefox add-on and a WordPress plugin. A white-label Pro embed option lets other sites add calculators.
