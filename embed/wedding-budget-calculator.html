Typical wedding budget splits

Planners commonly suggest splits like these. Every couple differs, so move money to what matters most to you. Percentages are of the amount left after the contingency.

How to plan a wedding budget

  • Enter your total budget and guest count.
  • Set a contingency of about 10%.
  • Choose an all-inclusive venue or bring-your-own vendors.
  • Use the split as a starting point and move money to what matters most.

The category shares are typical planning guidance, not fixed rules or official statistics. Costs vary widely by region, season and guest count. Get written quotes before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How much should a wedding cost?expand_more

It depends heavily on location and guest count. Recent US surveys put the average around $30,000 to $36,000, but many weddings cost far less.

How much should I set aside for contingency?expand_more

About 10% is common. It covers price rises, extra guests, overtime and surprise fees.

What is the biggest wedding cost?expand_more

The venue and catering, often 40% to 45% of the budget. Cutting the guest list is the most effective way to cut costs.

How are vendor payments usually scheduled?expand_more

A deposit at signing, often 25% to 50%, a second payment weeks before, and the balance on or just before the day. Check each contract.

What if I bring my own vendors?expand_more

You may save on catering markups but pay for rentals, staffing and coordination separately, so those get their own line here.
