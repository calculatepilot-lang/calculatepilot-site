How this calculator works

  • Net worth = total assets − total liabilities.
  • Debt-to-asset ratio = total liabilities ÷ total assets — lower means more of what you own is truly yours.
  • Liquid assets = cash + non-retirement investments you could access without penalties.
  • Expected net worth (rule of thumb) = age × pre-tax household income ÷ 10, from The Millionaire Next Door (Stanley & Danko). The authors call households at twice this figure or more "prodigious accumulators of wealth" and those at half or less "under accumulators".

A worked example

Using the default figures: assets of $15,000 + $25,000 + $60,000 + $350,000 + $18,000 = $468,000, and liabilities of $240,000 + $12,000 + $18,000 + $4,000 = $274,000, give a net worth of $194,000. That's a debt-to-asset ratio of 59%. For a 38-year-old, the SCF 2022 median for ages 35–44 is $135,600, so this household is above the median for its age group.

Why net worth matters more than income

Income measures cash flow; net worth measures your actual financial position. Someone with a high income but heavy debt and little saved can have a lower net worth than someone with a modest income and steady saving habits. Tracking it over time — quarterly or yearly — shows whether you're moving in the right direction. Home equity and retirement accounts count toward net worth but aren't easy to spend, which is why liquid assets are shown separately.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include my car's value as an asset?expand_more

You can include it for a complete picture — use its current resale value, not what you paid. Many people leave depreciating items like vehicles out when tracking wealth-building, since they lose value every year.

How often should I recalculate my net worth?expand_more

Many people track it quarterly or annually — often enough to see meaningful trends, but not so often that normal market swings feel misleading.

What is the median net worth in the US?expand_more

In the Federal Reserve's 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances, the median US family net worth was $192,900 and the mean was about $1.06 million. The mean is much higher because a small number of very wealthy families pull the average up.
