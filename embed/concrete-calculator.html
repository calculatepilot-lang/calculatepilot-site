Formulas

How the volume is calculated

All sizes are converted to feet first, then divided by 27 because one cubic yard is 27 cubic feet.

Slab or footing

yd³ = L × W × (T ÷ 12) ÷ 27 × (1 + waste)

Round column

ft³ = π × (d ÷ 24)² × h

Steps

ft³ = width × (run ÷ 12) × (rise ÷ 12) × n(n + 1) ÷ 2

One cubic yard is 27 ft³ or 46,656 in³. Normalweight concrete weighs about 145 lb per cubic foot (roughly 3,900 lb per yd³).

Strength

Common compressive strengths

2,500 psi · low-traffic residential. Interior floors, footings, small pads and garden walks.
3,000 – 3,500 psi · standard exterior. Driveways, patios and garage aprons.
4,000+ psi · heavy or freeze-thaw exposure. Loading docks and equipment pads.

Check local code and your supplier, since required strength depends on climate, use and load.

Worked examples

Illustrative jobs, each checked by hand. The first is the default setup.

20 × 15 ft patio, 4 in

10% waste allowance.

20 × 15 × 4/12 = 100 ft³
× 1.10 = 110 ft³

12 in diameter, 4 ft deep, no waste.

radius = 0.5 ft
π × 0.5² × 4 = 3.14 ft³
3.14 ÷ 0.60 = 5.2 → 6 bags (80 lb)

7 in rise, 11 in run, 4 ft wide.

n(n+1)/2 = 10
4 ft × (11/12) ft × (7/12) ft × 10
= 21.39 ft³ = 0.79 yd³

How much concrete do I need?

  • Measure the length, width and thickness of the pour, or the size of the footing, column or steps.
  • Add a waste allowance of about 5% to 10% for uneven ground and spillage, and 10% to 15% for rough subgrades.
  • Under about 1 cubic yard, premix bags are practical. Above that, ready-mix is usually easier and gives a more even pour.
  • Order ready-mix by the cubic yard and ask about any minimum-load or short-load charge.

This estimate covers the concrete only. It does not include gravel base, forms, reinforcement or finishing, and slabs may need thicker edges.

Frequently asked questions

How many 80 lb bags make a cubic yard?expand_more

An 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 cubic feet, so one cubic yard (27 ft³) needs about 45 bags. A 60 lb bag yields about 0.45 ft³ (60 bags per yard) and a 50 lb bag about 0.375 ft³ (72 bags per yard).

How much extra concrete should I order?expand_more

Most people add 5% to 10%. Use more for rough or uneven subgrade, deep forms or long trenches. Running short mid-pour can cause a cold joint, which is a weak seam in the slab.

Should I use bags or a ready-mix truck?expand_more

Bags suit small jobs such as post holes and small pads. Once a pour passes about a cubic yard, mixing by hand is slow and the mix can vary, so a truck is usually the better choice. Compare the totals above with your local quote.

How much does a cubic yard of concrete weigh?expand_more

Normalweight concrete weighs about 145 to 150 pounds per cubic foot, so roughly 3,900 to 4,050 pounds per cubic yard. This tool uses 145 lb/ft³ for the weight estimate.

How thick should a concrete slab be?expand_more

A 4 inch slab is common for patios and walkways. Driveways and garage floors are often 5 to 6 inches, and heavy loads need more. Confirm the thickness with your local building code or an engineer.
