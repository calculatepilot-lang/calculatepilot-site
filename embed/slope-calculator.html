    Types of slope

    How the sign and size of m describe a line.

    LineSlope mAngle θDescriptionEquation
    Positivem > 00° < θ < 90°Rises from left to righty = mx + b
    Negativem < 0−90° < θ < 0°Falls from left to righty = mx + b
    Zero (horizontal)m = 0θ = 0°Perfectly levely = b
    Undefined (vertical)Δx = 0θ = 90°Straight up and downx = a
    Parallel lines have equal slopes. Perpendicular lines have slopes that multiply to −1 (m₁ × m₂ = −1), i.e. m₂ = −1 ÷ m₁.

    Slope in the real world

    accessible

    Wheelchair ramps

    US accessibility standards (ADA) set a maximum running slope of 1:12 for ramps.

    1 ÷ 12 = 0.0833 = 8.33% grade
    θ = arctan(1/12) = 4.76°
    add_road

    Road drainage

    Pavements are crowned or cambered so that water runs off; cross slopes of about 1.5–2% are common.

    2 ft drop over 100 ft = 2% = 1.15°
    roofing

    Roof pitch

    Pitch is quoted as rise per 12 units of run: “6 in 12” means 6 inches of rise per foot.

    6 ÷ 12 = 0.5 → 26.57°, 50% grade

    What is slope?

    Slope measures how steep a line is: the vertical change (rise) divided by the horizontal change (run) between any two points on it, m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). It is the same for every pair of points on a straight line. Slope is the tangent of the angle the line makes with the x-axis, m = tan θ.

    Three ways to write a line

    • Slope-intercept: y = mx + b, where b is the y-intercept.
    • Point-slope: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁), for a known point (x₁, y₁).
    • Standard form: Ax + By = C, with the slope −A ÷ B and y-intercept C ÷ B.

    Grade, angle and pitch

    Percent grade = slope × 100. The angle is arctan(slope). A roof pitch of “n in 12” has slope n ÷ 12. A 100% grade is a 45° incline.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is the slope of a vertical line undefined?expand_more

    A vertical line has Δx = 0, so the slope formula divides by zero. Its angle is 90° and its equation is x = a, rather than y = mx + b.

    How is slope related to the derivative?expand_more

    The derivative dy/dx is the slope of the tangent line to a curve at a point. For a straight line the slope is constant, so the derivative equals m everywhere.

    What is the difference between percent slope, degrees and roof pitch?expand_more

    They are the same steepness in different units. Percent slope is 100 × rise ÷ run; degrees are arctan(rise ÷ run); pitch is rise per 12 units of run. A 12% slope is 6.84°, or a pitch of 1.44 in 12. They are not proportional: 100% slope is 45°, not 100°.

    How do I find a perpendicular or parallel slope?expand_more

    A parallel line has the same slope m. A perpendicular line has slope −1 ÷ m (the negative reciprocal). If m = 0 the perpendicular line is vertical, and vice versa.

    How do I convert Ax + By = C to y = mx + b?expand_more

    Solve for y: y = (−A ÷ B)x + C ÷ B, provided B ≠ 0. If B = 0 the line is vertical at x = C ÷ A.

    Can slope be a fraction or a decimal?expand_more

    Yes. Slope is any real number. It is often best left as an exact fraction such as 2/3, because the decimal 0.6667 is only an approximation.
