    What is a fraction?

    A fraction a/b represents a parts out of b equal parts, or the quotient a ÷ b. It is proper if a < b, improper if a ≥ b, and a mixed number combines a whole number with a proper fraction (1 5/12 = 17/12). Two fractions are equivalent if they have the same value: 2/4 = 1/2.

    How to simplify

    Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor (GCD), found with Euclid’s algorithm. For 17/12: GCD(17, 12) = GCD(12, 5) = GCD(5, 2) = GCD(2, 1) = 1, so 17/12 is already in lowest terms.

    Repeating decimals

    A fraction in lowest terms has a terminating decimal exactly when its denominator has no prime factors other than 2 and 5. Otherwise the decimal repeats; the repeating block is at most (denominator − 1) digits long. For example 17/12 = 1.41666… = 1.41(6).

    Frequently asked questions

    Why use the lowest common denominator instead of just multiplying the denominators?expand_more

    Any common denominator works, but the lowest one keeps the numbers small and often skips a later simplification step. For 5/6 + 1/4, multiplying gives 26/24 which must be reduced to 13/12; the LCD 12 gives 13/12 directly.

    How can I tell whether a decimal will terminate or repeat?expand_more

    Reduce the fraction, then factor the denominator. If it contains only 2s and 5s, the decimal terminates (3/8 = 0.375). Any other prime factor makes it repeat (1/3 = 0.(3), 1/7 = 0.(142857)).

    How do negative fractions work?expand_more

    Only the fraction as a whole carries a sign: −3/4 = 3/(−4) = −(3/4). This calculator keeps the denominator positive and puts the sign on the numerator (or in front of a mixed number).

    How do I convert a mixed number to an improper fraction?expand_more

    Multiply the whole number by the denominator, add the numerator, and keep the denominator: 2 1/3 = (2 × 3 + 1)/3 = 7/3. Going the other way, divide and keep the remainder: 7/3 = 2 remainder 1, so 2 1/3.

    Why can’t the denominator be zero?expand_more

    Division by zero is undefined. A denominator of 0 would mean splitting something into zero parts. Dividing by a fraction whose numerator is 0 is likewise undefined.

    What is a continued fraction?expand_more

    It writes a number as a whole part plus 1 over another whole part plus 1 over …: 17/12 = 1 + 1/(2 + 1/(2 + 1/2)), written [1; 2, 2, 2]. The quotients are exactly the ones produced by Euclid’s algorithm.
