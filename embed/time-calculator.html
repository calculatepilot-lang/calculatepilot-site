Minutes to decimal hours

Payroll usually needs time as hours in decimals. Divide the minutes by 60.

The 7-minute rounding rule. Where an employer rounds to the nearest quarter hour, 1 to 7 minutes round down and 8 to 14 minutes round up to the next quarter. So 8 to 22 minutes count as 15 minutes, 23 to 37 as 30, 38 to 52 as 45 and 53 to 60 as a full hour. Rounding must average out so employees are paid for all time worked.

Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

schedule

Time between two clocks

Convert both times to minutes since midnight, add a day offset if the end is on a later day, and subtract.

elapsed = (end + 1440 × days) − start
net = elapsed − break
add_circle

Add or subtract time

Add or subtract the span in minutes, then wrap around 24 hours to get the clock time and the day shift.

result = start ± (days × 1440 + hours × 60 + minutes)
pin

Decimal hours

Minutes divided by 60. Pay is decimal hours times the hourly rate.

decimal hours = minutes ÷ 60
pay = decimal hours × rate

Worked examples

Illustrative figures, each checked by hand. The first is the default setup.

Shift with a break

8:45 AM to 5:15 PM, 45 minute unpaid break, $30 an hour (the default).

elapsed = 17:15 − 08:45 = 8 h 30 m
net = 8 h 30 m − 45 m = 7 h 45 m = 7.75 h
pay = 7.75 × $30 = $232.50

Night shift

10:00 PM to 6:30 AM the next day, 30 minute break.

(6:30 + 24 h) − 22:00 = 8 h 30 m
net = 8 h 00 m = 8.00 h

Add time

9:00 AM plus 2 hours 45 minutes.

9:00 + 2:45 = 11:45 AM the same day

How to calculate time

  • Choose whether to find the time between clocks, add or subtract, or add up durations.

  • Add the unpaid break and an hourly rate if you want pay.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate time between two times?expand_more

Convert each to minutes since midnight and subtract. If the end is earlier than the start the span crosses midnight, so add 24 hours to the end.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?expand_more

Divide the minutes by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours, and 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours.

What is the 7-minute rule?expand_more

A payroll method that rounds working time to the nearest quarter hour. Up to 7 minutes rounds down, 8 minutes or more rounds up.

How do I subtract time?expand_more

Choose Subtract, enter the hours and minutes to take away, and the result shows the earlier clock time and how many days back it falls.

Does it handle daylight saving?expand_more

No. It does clock arithmetic on a 24-hour day, so a shift across a daylight saving change will be an hour out.
