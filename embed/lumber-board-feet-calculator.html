Formulas

How board feet are calculated

A board foot is a volume of 144 cubic inches, the size of a board 1 in thick, 12 in wide and 12 in long.

Length in feet

BF = T (in) × W (in) × L (ft) ÷ 12

Length in inches

BF = T × W × L (in) ÷ 144

With waste

BF gross = BF net × (1 + waste ÷ 100)
Hardwood thickness

Quarters explained

Rough hardwood is sold by thickness in quarters of an inch: 4/4 is 1 in, 5/4 is 1.25 in, 6/4 is 1.5 in and 8/4 is 2 in. Dried and planed boards finish thinner than the rough size.

Prices are per board foot and depend on species and grade. Ask the mill whether the price is for rough or surfaced stock.

What is a board foot?

  • One board foot equals 144 cubic inches of wood: 1 in × 12 in × 12 in.
  • Multiply thickness by width in inches and length in feet, then divide by 12.
  • Add 10% to 25% for defects, cutting loss and mistakes, more for knotty or lower-grade stock.
  • Multiply by the price per board foot to get the cost.

Board feet measure volume, while linear feet measure length only. Big-box stores often price framing lumber by the piece or linear foot, so convert before comparing to a sawmill's board-foot price.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate board feet?expand_more

Multiply thickness in inches by width in inches by length in feet, then divide by 12. A 2 × 4 that is 8 feet long is 2 × 4 × 8 ÷ 12 = 5.33 board feet.

Do I use nominal or actual sizes?expand_more

Lumber is priced on the nominal size, so a 2 × 4 counts as 2 × 4 inches even though it measures 1.5 × 3.5 inches. Hardwood is priced on its rough thickness in quarters. This tool follows those trade conventions.

How much waste should I allow?expand_more

About 10% for clean, straight stock and 15% for typical work. Rustic or knotty boards, short cut-offs and complex joinery can push it to 25% or more.

What is the difference between board feet and linear feet?expand_more

Linear feet count only length. Board feet count volume, so a wide thick board has more board feet than a narrow thin one of the same length.

How many board feet are in a cubic foot?expand_more

There are 12 board feet in a cubic foot, because a cubic foot is 1,728 cubic inches and a board foot is 144.
