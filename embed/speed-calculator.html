Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

speed

Speed

Distance divided by time. Time and distance follow by rearranging.

speed = distance ÷ time
time = distance ÷ speed
distance = speed × time
timer

Pace

Pace is time per unit distance, the inverse of speed.

pace per mile = 60 ÷ mph minutes
pace per km = 60 ÷ km/h minutes
swap_horiz

Unit conversions

Exact conversion factors.

1 mile = 1,609.344 m
1 knot = 1.852 km/h
1 m/s = 3.6 km/h

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate speed?expand_more

Divide the distance by the time taken. 26.2 miles in 3.5 hours is 7.49 mph.

How do I convert pace to speed?expand_more

Divide 60 by the pace in minutes per mile to get mph. An 8:00 mile is 60 ÷ 8 = 7.5 mph.

How long is a marathon?expand_more

42.195 kilometres, which is 26.2 miles (26 miles 385 yards).

What is a knot?expand_more

One nautical mile per hour, or 1.852 km/h. It is used at sea and in aviation.

Is average speed the same as average of speeds?expand_more

No. Average speed is total distance divided by total time. Driving out at 60 mph and back at 40 mph averages 48 mph, not 50.
