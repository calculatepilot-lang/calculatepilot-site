Formulas

How the volume is calculated

Area in square feet times depth in feet gives cubic feet. Divide by 27 for cubic yards.

Rectangle

ft³ = L × W × (depth ÷ 12)

Circle

ft³ = π × (d ÷ 2)² × (depth ÷ 12)

Order size

yd³ = ft³ × (1 + settling) ÷ 27
Typical weights

Approximate weight per cubic yard

Shredded hardwood mulch · about 750 lb
Pine bark nuggets or straw · about 550 lb
Screened topsoil · about 2,200 lb
Compost · about 1,300 lb
Pea gravel or river stone · about 2,700 lb

Wet material can weigh far more. Ask your supplier for the actual load weight before towing.

How much mulch do I need?

  • Measure the bed and work out its area, or enter a known area in square feet.
  • Pick a depth. Two to three inches of mulch controls weeds without smothering roots.
  • Choose the material and a settling allowance, since mulch and compost compact over time.
  • Buy in bulk for beds over about a cubic yard and in bags for small jobs.

Keep mulch a few inches away from tree trunks and house walls. Deep piles against bark can cause rot.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a cubic yard of mulch cover?expand_more

One cubic yard (27 cubic feet) covers about 162 square feet at 2 inches deep, 108 square feet at 3 inches, and 324 square feet at 1 inch.

How deep should mulch be?expand_more

Two to three inches is typical for flower and shrub beds. Use about one inch when refreshing existing mulch. Very deep layers can hold too much moisture against plant stems.

Is bulk or bagged mulch cheaper?expand_more

Bulk is usually cheaper per cubic foot once you need more than about a cubic yard, but you must collect it or pay for delivery. Enter your local prices above to compare the two.

Why add a settling allowance?expand_more

Fresh mulch and compost compress and break down. Adding 5% to 10% means the bed is still at the right depth after it settles.

Will a pickup truck carry a cubic yard?expand_more

Often yes for light mulch, but soil and stone are heavy. A full yard of topsoil or gravel can be well over a ton, so check your truck's payload rating.
