Healthy Weight Range Calculator

Calculate the healthy weight range for your height, based on the standard BMI range of 18.5–24.9.

How this range is calculated

This calculator works backward from the standard 'normal weight' BMI range (18.5 to 24.9) to find the corresponding weight range for your specific height — essentially showing you the weight window that would keep your BMI within that commonly used reference range.

Because it's derived directly from BMI, this range carries the same limitations as BMI itself — it doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat mass, so very muscular individuals may fall 'above range' while still being at a healthy body composition, and the reverse can be true for people with low muscle mass.

Using this as one reference point, not a strict target

This range is a population-level statistical reference, not a personalized medical target — factors like frame size, muscle mass, age, and overall health picture all matter alongside where your weight falls in this range. It's best used as a general orientation point rather than a strict goal to hit exactly.

For a more complete picture of healthy weight for your individual situation, pairing this with other measures (like waist circumference or body composition) — and ideally input from a healthcare provider — gives a fuller picture than any single number alone.

Frequently asked questions

Does this account for muscle mass?

No — like standard BMI, this range doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat, so very muscular individuals may fall above this range while still being at a healthy body composition.

Should I try to hit the exact middle of this range?

Not necessarily — this range is a general statistical reference, not a personalized target. Where you sit within (or even slightly outside) this range depends on individual factors like frame size and muscle mass.
