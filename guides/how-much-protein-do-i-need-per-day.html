How Much Protein Do You Need Per Day?

Your target depends on body weight and activity. Here is the formula, a chart by weight and a worked example.

→ Use the free Protein Calculator

Short answer: multiply your body weight in kilograms by a factor that depends on your activity. For a moderately active adult the range is 1.2 to 1.6 grams per kilogram a day, so an 82 kg person needs roughly 98–131 g. The 0.8 g/kg RDA is the minimum to prevent deficiency, not a target.

Protein (g) = Weight (kg) × Factor
Sedentary ≈ 0.8–1.0 · Adults 65+ ≈ 1.0–1.2 · Moderately active ≈ 1.2–1.6 · Athletes ≈ 1.6–2.2 g/kg

To convert pounds to kilograms, divide by 2.2046.

Worked example

  1. An 82 kg, moderately active person: 82 × 1.2 = 98 g to 82 × 1.6 = 131 g a day.
  2. The same person training seriously: 82 × 1.6 = 131 g to 82 × 2.2 = 180 g a day.
  3. That is nearly 50 g more at the top of the range.

For comparison, the calculator cites the Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025–2030, which set a general protein goal of 1.2–1.6 g per kg a day, above the 0.8 g/kg RDA.

How to spread protein across the day

Many guidelines suggest spreading intake over three or four meals with about 20–40 g each, roughly 0.4 g per kg of body weight per meal (33 g for an 82 kg person), rather than eating it all in one meal. Needs also tend to rise during calorie restriction to help preserve muscle, which is why the upper end of a range is often recommended while dieting.

Protein quality

Complete proteins contain all the essential amino acids. They are found in most animal products and soy, and in some plant combinations such as rice and beans. They are generally considered more effective for maintaining muscle than incomplete sources alone.

Common mistakes

  • Using pounds as if they were kilograms. Divide pounds by 2.2046 first.
  • Treating the RDA as a goal. 0.8 g/kg is a minimum to prevent deficiency.
  • Ignoring health conditions. People with kidney disease should follow their care team's advice on protein.

Frequently asked questions

How much protein do I need per day?

It depends on weight and activity: about 0.8–1.0 g/kg if sedentary, 1.2–1.6 g/kg if moderately active and 1.6–2.2 g/kg for athletes.

How much protein does an 82 kg person need?

Roughly 98–131 g a day if moderately active, or 131–180 g a day when training as an athlete.

Is the RDA the right target?

The 0.8 g/kg RDA is the minimum to prevent deficiency rather than a target. Many guidelines suggest more for active people.

How should I split protein across meals?

Many guidelines suggest three or four meals with roughly 20–40 g each, about 0.4 g per kg of body weight per meal.

Sources

  • Recommended Dietary Allowance for protein: 0.8 g per kg of body weight
