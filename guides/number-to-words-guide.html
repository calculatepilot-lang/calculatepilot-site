Number to Words Converter: How to Spell Numbers in Words

Learn how numbers are written in English and how to convert numeric amounts into words for checks, forms, invoices, and formal documents.

→ Use the free Number to Words Calculator

What is a number to words converter?

A number to words converter changes a numeric value such as 1,234 into its written English form: one thousand two hundred thirty-four. This is useful when preparing checks, contracts, invoices, applications, educational exercises, and other documents where a number may need to appear in words.

How to convert numbers to words

English number names are easiest to understand in groups of three digits. Start from the right and separate a large number into groups: ones, thousands, millions, billions, and so on.

Example: 12,345,678 = twelve million three hundred forty-five thousand six hundred seventy-eight.

1. Convert each three-digit group

For 5,214, read 5 as five thousand and 214 as two hundred fourteen. Combine the groups from left to right.

2. Add scale words

The main scale words are thousand, million, billion, and trillion. Each new group of three digits increases the scale by one level.

3. Use hyphens for compound numbers

Numbers from 21 through 99 that are not multiples of ten are commonly hyphenated: twenty-one, thirty-five, sixty-eight, and ninety-nine.

Writing amounts on checks

When a bank or organization asks for an amount in words, the written amount should agree exactly with the numeric amount. For example, $125.50 may be written as “one hundred twenty-five and 50/100 dollars,” depending on the format required by the institution. Always follow the instructions printed on the check or provided by your bank.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting the scale word, such as thousand or million.
  • Adding a scale word when its group is zero.
  • Using inconsistent hyphenation in compound numbers.
  • Failing to check that the written amount matches the digits.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert a number to words?

Separate the number into three-digit groups, convert each group, and attach the correct scale name such as thousand or million.

How do you write 1,234 in words?

One thousand two hundred thirty-four.

Can I use this for check writing?

Yes. Use the converter as a drafting aid, then verify the result against your bank's required format.
