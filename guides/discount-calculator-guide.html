Discount Calculator Guide: Calculate Sale Price & Savings

Learn how to calculate a percentage discount, final sale price, total savings, and discount-plus-tax amounts.

→ Use the free Discount Calculator

How to calculate a discount

To find the discount amount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage written as a decimal.

Discount = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)
Sale Price = Original Price − Discount

Example: 20% off $80

The discount is $80 × 0.20 = $16. The sale price is $80 − $16 = $64. You save $16.

How to calculate the final price after a discount

A faster method is to subtract the discount rate from 100%. For 20% off, you pay 80% of the original price: $80 × 0.80 = $64.

Discount plus sales tax

If tax is charged after the discount, calculate the sale price first, then apply the tax. For example, a $100 item with 20% off costs $80 before tax. With 8% tax, the final amount is $80 × 1.08 = $86.40.

How to find the original price from a sale price

If you know the discounted price and discount rate, divide by the percentage you actually paid. A $72 price after 20% off means you paid 80%, so $72 ÷ 0.80 = $90 original price.

Common mistakes

  • Applying the percentage to the already-discounted price when the discount is based on the original price.
  • Forgetting that tax rules vary and may be applied before or after discounts.
  • Rounding too early in a multi-step calculation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 0.20 to find the savings, then subtract that amount from the original price.

What is the sale price of $100 after 30% off?

The savings are $30, so the sale price is $70 before any applicable tax.

How do I calculate price with discount?

Multiply the original price by 1 − discount rate. For 25% off, multiply by 0.75.
