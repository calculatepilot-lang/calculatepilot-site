How movers calculate their price

Local movers charge by the hour: typically $25–$50 per mover, billed as a team. Two movers with a truck cost $100–$200/hour. Three movers cost $150–$250/hour. Most companies have a minimum of 2–4 hours.

Long-distance movers charge by weight and distance. Average household goods weigh 3,000–7,000 lbs for a 2–3 bedroom home.

What makes moves more expensive

Timing: Moving in June–August costs 20–40% more. Month-end weekends are also pricier. Tuesday–Thursday moves in October–March get the lowest rates.

Stairs: Most movers charge $50–$75 extra per flight of stairs.

Long carry: If the truck can't park within 75 feet, expect a $50–$100+ surcharge.

Specialty items: Pianos cost $150–$350 extra. Hot tubs and pool tables cost $200–$600.

How to save money on your move

  • Move off-peak: Mid-week, mid-month moves in fall or winter are consistently cheaper
  • Declutter first: Less weight = lower cost on long-distance moves
  • Pack yourself: Self-packing saves 30–50% vs full-service
  • Get 3 binding quotes: Prices vary significantly between companies

How much does it cost to move a 3-bedroom house?

A 3-bedroom local move typically costs $1,200–$3,500 with professional movers. A long-distance 3-bedroom move costs $3,500–$8,000+ depending on distance.

Is it cheaper to rent a truck or hire movers?

Renting a truck yourself costs $200–$800 for a local move — 50–70% cheaper than hiring movers. PODS are a middle-ground at $1,000–$3,000 for long distance.
