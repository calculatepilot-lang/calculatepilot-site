How movers calculate their price

Local movers charge by the hour: typically $25–$50 per mover, billed as a team. Two movers with a truck cost $100–$200/hour. Three movers cost $150–$250/hour. Most companies have a minimum charge of 2–4 hours.

Long-distance movers charge by weight and distance. The standard formula is: price per pound × total weight + fuel surcharge + additional services. Average household goods weigh 3,000–7,000 lbs for a 2–3 bedroom home.

What makes moves more expensive

Timing: Moving in June–August (peak season) costs 20–40% more. Month-end weekends (last 3 days of the month) are also more expensive. Moving on a Tuesday–Thursday in October–March will get you the lowest rates.

Stairs and elevators: Most movers charge $50–$75 extra per flight of stairs. Elevators that require reservations or have size restrictions also add fees.

Long carry distance: If the truck can't park close to your home (more than 75 feet away), expect a long-carry surcharge of $50–$100+.

Specialty items: Pianos cost $150–$350 extra. Hot tubs and pool tables cost $200–$600. Gun safes and large artwork require special handling.

How to save money on your move

  • Move off-peak: Mid-week, mid-month moves in fall or winter are consistently cheaper
  • Declutter first: Less weight = lower cost on long-distance moves
  • Pack yourself: Self-packing saves 30–50% vs full-service
  • Get at least 3 binding quotes: Prices vary significantly between companies
  • Check FMCSA registration: All interstate movers must be licensed. Verify at protectyourmove.gov

Moving cost FAQs

How much does it cost to hire movers for a local move?

Local moves (under 50 miles) cost $800–$2,500 for a 2-bedroom home, based on hourly rates of $100–$200 for 2 movers plus a truck. A studio apartment costs $300–$800. A 4-bedroom house costs $2,500–$5,000.

What is the average cost of long-distance moving?

Long-distance moves (over 100 miles) average $2,000–$7,500 for a 2–3 bedroom home, with cross-country moves costing $5,000–$10,000+. The price depends primarily on weight and total distance.

Is it cheaper to rent a truck or hire movers?

Renting a truck yourself costs $200–$800 for a local move plus fuel, which is 50–70% cheaper than hiring movers. However, you do all the physical work. For long-distance moves, portable storage containers (PODS) are a middle-ground option at $1,000–$3,000.
