What is the monthly payment on a $30,000 student loan?

At 6.5% over 10 years: $340/month, with $10,800 total interest. At the current federal rate of 5.5%: $326/month.

Is it better to pay off student loans early?

Yes. On a $30,000 loan at 6.5%, paying $100 extra/month saves ~$2,800 in interest and shortens repayment by 2.5 years. Federal loans have no prepayment penalty.
