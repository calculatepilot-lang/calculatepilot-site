Moving Cost Calculator: Estimate Your Moving Costs

Estimate the major costs of a move before you request quotes or book a moving service.

CalculatePilot's Moving Cost Calculator is designed to help you build a realistic moving budget. A useful estimate should consider more than a single hourly labor figure: distance, home size, labor hours, packing, storage, supplies and special services can all change the final cost.

What affects moving costs?

  • Move distance: local and long-distance moves are priced differently.
  • Home size: more rooms generally mean more items, packing and labor.
  • Labor: hourly movers may charge based on crew size and time.
  • Packing: boxes, protective materials and professional packing add cost.
  • Storage: temporary storage can become a significant part of the budget.
  • Special items: pianos, large appliances and fragile items may require additional handling.

How to estimate a local move

For a local move, start with an estimated crew size and number of labor hours. Add truck or travel charges, packing materials and optional services. Then compare the result with written quotes from moving companies.

How to estimate a long-distance move

Long-distance pricing can depend on distance, shipment size, weight, origin, destination and service level. Use the calculator as a planning estimate rather than a binding quote.

Moving cost by number of bedrooms

Bedroom count is only a proxy for shipment size. Two homes with the same number of bedrooms can have very different moving costs because furniture, storage, packing and household contents vary. Use the bedroom count as a starting point and adjust for the amount of stuff you actually have.

Don't forget hidden moving expenses

Commonly overlooked costs include packing supplies, cleaning, temporary accommodation, storage, elevator reservations, parking permits, travel meals, insurance or valuation coverage and replacing items that are cheaper to buy than move.

How to use a moving estimate responsibly

Run a low, expected and high scenario. Then request several quotes and compare what each quote includes. The calculator is most useful for establishing a budget range before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a move cost?

There is no single price. The biggest variables are distance, shipment size, labor, packing and optional services.

Is a calculator quote binding?

No. Treat an online estimate as a planning range and compare it with a written quote from a moving provider.

How can I lower moving costs?

Declutter before the move, compare quotes, avoid unnecessary premium services and pack items yourself when practical.
