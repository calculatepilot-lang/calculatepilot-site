Do cars depreciate faster in the first year?expand_more

Yes. The moment a new car leaves the dealership it loses 10–15% immediately. By end of year one, total depreciation is typically 20–30%. Buying a 1–2 year old used car avoids this initial drop.

Does high mileage increase depreciation?expand_more

Yes. The standard assumption is 12,000–15,000 miles per year. Each 10,000 miles above average typically reduces resale value by $500–$1,500.
