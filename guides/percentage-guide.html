How to Calculate Percentages — Complete Guide with Examples

Three types of percentage calculations explained with formulas and real examples. Use our free calculator to get instant answers.

Three percentage formulas:
1. X% of a number: Number × (X ÷ 100)
2. What % is A of B: (A ÷ B) × 100
3. % change: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Common percentage shortcuts

  • 10% — divide by 10 (e.g. 10% of 350 = 35)
  • 5% — find 10% then halve it (e.g. 5% of 350 = 17.5)
  • 20% — divide by 5 (e.g. 20% of 350 = 70)
  • 25% — divide by 4 (e.g. 25% of 350 = 87.5)
  • 50% — divide by 2 (e.g. 50% of 350 = 175)
  • 1% — divide by 100, then multiply any % from there

How do I calculate 15% of something quickly?

Find 10% (divide by 10), then add half of that for 5%. Add them together. Example: 15% of $80 = $8 (10%) + $4 (5%) = $12.

What is 20% of a number?

Divide the number by 5. Example: 20% of $250 = $250 ÷ 5 = $50. Alternatively, multiply by 0.20.

How do I add a percentage to a price?

Multiply by (1 + percentage as decimal). Adding 20% VAT to £100: £100 × 1.20 = £120. Adding 15% tip to a $45 restaurant bill: $45 × 1.15 = $51.75.

How do I reverse a percentage (remove it from a total)?

Divide by (1 + percentage as decimal). To find the pre-VAT price when the VAT-inclusive price is £120 at 20% VAT: £120 ÷ 1.20 = £100.
