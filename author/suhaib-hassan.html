Suhaib Hassan

Founder, CalculatePilot

Suhaib Hassan is the founder of CalculatePilot, a free calculator platform covering finance, health, math, and everyday tools. He writes guides covering how financial, health, and economic news developments — rate changes, tax updates, retirement rule changes, inflation data — affect the numbers CalculatePilot's calculators produce.

Coverage areas: mortgage and loan rates, tax and paycheck changes, retirement account rules, inflation and cost-of-living data, and health and fitness metric updates as they relate to CalculatePilot's tools.
