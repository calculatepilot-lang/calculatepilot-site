How this calculator worksFIXED-RATE AMORTIZATION

This calculator uses the standard fixed-rate amortization formula for principal and interest, then adds property tax and home insurance so you see a housing cost — not only the loan payment.

M = P × [ i(1+i)^n ] / [ (1+i)^n − 1 ]

Loan amount is home price minus down payment. Estimated PMI of 0.50%/year appears only below 20% down.

What goes into a monthly mortgage payment

Most lenders quote PITI: principal, interest, taxes, and insurance.

Principal

The portion of each payment that reduces the outstanding balance.

Interest

The cost of borrowing. Early 30-year payments are mostly interest.

Property Tax

County assessments collected monthly into escrow.

Homeowners Insurance

Hazard coverage, often escrowed with taxes.

PMI Considerations

Required below 20% down. Typically 0.3–1.5% of the loan per year. This page estimates 0.50%/year.

HOA Dues & Fees

Set by the association, not the lender. Enter monthly HOA above.

Biweekly Payment Power

26 half-payments equal 13 full payments and can shorten a 30-year loan by about 4–6 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a monthly mortgage payment calculated?

Fixed-rate amortization for principal and interest, then monthly tax and insurance. Estimate only — not a lender quote.

Does this include PMI, HOA fees, or closing costs?

HOA is optional. Estimated PMI appears below 20% down. Closing costs are not included.

How much house can I afford?

A common starting point is 28–36% of gross income. Use the House Affordability Calculator.

Should I choose a 15-year or 30-year mortgage?

15-year usually costs less interest and more per month. 30-year keeps the payment lower.

Does a larger down payment lower my monthly payment?

Yes. It reduces the loan amount and often removes PMI at 20% down.

Are these figures a formal loan offer?

No. Confirm rates and fees with a lender quote.

Should I pay extra on my mortgage or invest that money instead?

On a $400,000 loan at 7%, $300/month extra saves $168,391 in interest and pays off 7.8 years early — a guaranteed 7% return. Investing the same $300/month at 8% would grow to ~$450,389 over the same period. See the full extra-payments-vs-investing breakdown for the math.

When exactly does PMI come off my loan?

By federal law, PMI automatically ends at 78% of your home's original value; you can request removal yourself at 80%. See worked PMI-removal timing examples including how extra payments accelerate it.
