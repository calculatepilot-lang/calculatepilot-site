How this calculator works

Fixed-rate amortization for principal and interest, plus monthly tax, insurance, optional HOA and estimated PMI below 20% down.

M = P × [ i(1+i)^n ] / [ (1+i)^n − 1 ]

Frequently asked questions

How is a monthly mortgage payment calculated?

Fixed-rate amortization, then monthly tax and insurance. Estimate only.

Does this include PMI or HOA?

HOA is optional. PMI is estimated below 20% down. Closing costs are not included.

15-year or 30-year?

15-year costs less interest and more per month. Compare the cards above.
