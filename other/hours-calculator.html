The 7-minute rounding rule

Under 29 CFR 785.48(b), employers may round punches to the nearest five minutes, tenth or quarter hour if the rounding averages out over time and does not favour the employer.

Formulas

How hours and pay are worked out

Net hours

(out − in) − (meal in − meal out), plus 24 h if the shift crosses midnight

Overtime (weekly)

regular = min(hours, 40); overtime = hours − 40

Gross pay

pay = regular × rate + overtime × rate × 1.5 + double time × rate × 2
Rules

Overtime varies by place

Federal law requires 1.5× pay after 40 hours in a workweek for covered, non-exempt employees. Some states add daily overtime, and California also pays double time after 12 hours in a day and has special rules for the seventh consecutive day. This tool does not apply that seventh-day rule.

Bona fide meal periods of 30 minutes or more are generally unpaid if you are fully relieved of duty (29 CFR 785.19).

How to calculate hours worked

  • Record the time you clocked in and out each day, and any unpaid meal break.
  • Subtract the break from the time between in and out.
  • Add the daily hours, then apply your overtime rule to the week to find regular and overtime hours.
  • Multiply by your rates to get gross pay, and subtract taxes to estimate take-home.

Payroll rules depend on your employer, state and job type. Exempt employees are not entitled to overtime, so check your status before relying on the numbers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate hours worked with a lunch break?expand_more

Subtract the clock-in time from the clock-out time and then subtract the unpaid break. From 8:30 to 17:15 with a 45 minute lunch is 8 hours 45 minutes minus 45 minutes, which is 8.00 hours.

How do I turn minutes into a decimal?expand_more

Divide the minutes by 60. Fifteen minutes is 0.25 hours, 30 minutes is 0.50, 45 minutes is 0.75 and 20 minutes is 0.33 hours.

How is overtime calculated?expand_more

Under federal law, non-exempt employees earn at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. Some states also count hours over 8 in a day. Choose the rule that applies to you.

What is the 7-minute rule?expand_more

It is a common way of rounding time clocks to the nearest quarter hour: punches 1 to 7 minutes past a quarter round down and 8 to 14 minutes round up. Rounding is allowed if it averages out fairly over time.

Are meal breaks paid?expand_more

Generally not, if the break is at least 30 minutes and you are completely relieved of duty. Short rest breaks of about 5 to 20 minutes are normally paid under federal rules.
