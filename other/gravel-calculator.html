Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

straighten

Volume

Length times width times depth in feet, divided by 27 for cubic yards. In inches of depth: L × W × D ÷ 324.

yd³ = L × W × (D ÷ 12) ÷ 27
scale

Weight

Cubic yards times the stone density, with a compaction allowance added on top.

tons = yd³ × lb per yd³ × (1 + allowance) ÷ 2,000
shopping_bag

Bags

Total pounds divided by the bag size, rounded up.

bags = ⌈ pounds ÷ 50 ⌉

How much gravel do I need?

  • Measure the length and width of the area, or enter its total square feet.
  • Choose the depth. Four inches is common for driveways and two to three for paths.
  • Pick the stone, or enter the density from your quarry ticket.
  • Add a compaction allowance of about 10% for a compacted base, then compare bulk delivery with bags.

Stone weighs different amounts depending on size, moisture and quarry. For a large order, ask your supplier for the weight per cubic yard and how they load, since they sell by the ton.

Frequently asked questions

How many tons are in a cubic yard of gravel?expand_more

It depends on the stone, but most crushed stone weighs about 1.3 to 1.5 tons per cubic yard (2,600 to 3,000 pounds). This tool uses about 1.35 tons for #57 stone and lets you enter your own density.

How deep should a gravel driveway be?expand_more

A common build is a 4 to 6 inch compacted base of crusher run topped with 1.5 to 2 inches of #57 stone. Soft soil or heavy vehicles need more, so check local guidance.

Why add a compaction allowance?expand_more

Loose stone settles and compacts once it is spread and driven on. Ordering 5% to 15% extra means the finished layer reaches the depth you planned.

Is bulk delivery cheaper than bags?expand_more

For anything over about half a ton, bulk is almost always cheaper per ton, even after a delivery fee. Bags suit small repairs. Enter your prices to compare.

What size dump truck do I need?expand_more

A tandem dump truck typically carries about 10 to 14 tons, so most driveways fit in one load. The tool shows how many loads your total needs.
