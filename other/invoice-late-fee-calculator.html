Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

event

Days charged interest

Days past the due date, less any grace period. Interest only runs after the grace period.

days late = as-of date − due date
charged days = max(0, days late − grace)
percent

Simple interest

Amount times the yearly rate times the fraction of a year.

interest = amount × rate × days ÷ 365
trending_up

Compound interest

Interest added to the balance monthly or yearly.

monthly: amount × ((1 + rate ÷ 12)12 × days ÷ 365 − 1)
yearly: amount × ((1 + rate)days ÷ 365 − 1)

Illustrative figures, each checked by hand. The first is the default setup.

Default

$8,500 due 15 August, calculated to 14 September, 5 grace days, 18% simple, $50 fee.

days past due = 30, charged days = 25
interest = 8,500 × 0.18 × 25 ÷ 365 = $104.79
total = 8,500 + 104.79 + 50 = $8,654.79

Per-day interest

Same invoice.

How to calculate a late fee

  • Enter the invoice amount and its due date.
  • Set the date to calculate up to and any grace period from your terms.
  • Enter the yearly interest rate and whether it compounds, plus any flat fee.
  • Read the total owed and copy the summary for your reminder.

Not legal advice. Late fees and interest are only enforceable where your contract or the law allows them, and some places cap the rate. Check the terms and local rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can I charge a late fee on any invoice?expand_more

Only if your contract or invoice terms say so, or a law gives you the right, such as the UK and EU late payment rules for business-to-business debts.

What interest rate is reasonable?expand_more

Many businesses use 1% to 1.5% a month (12% to 18% a year) if their terms allow it. Some places cap the rate, and statutory rates differ.

What is a grace period?expand_more

A number of days after the due date during which no interest or fee is charged. It is set in your terms.

Simple or compound interest?expand_more

Simple interest is charged on the original amount only and is the norm for invoices. Compound interest adds unpaid interest to the balance.

Is the day the invoice is paid included?expand_more

This counts the days between the due date and the date you choose, not including the due date itself.
