Military time zone letters

Each hour of offset from UTC has a letter, said with the NATO phonetic word. Zulu (Z) is UTC. "1430Z" means 14:30 UTC. The letter J is not used, and only whole-hour zones have a letter.

Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

public

Convert to UTC

Subtract the zone's offset (including daylight saving on that date) from the local time.

UTC = local time − offset
tag

Unix time and Julian day

Unix time counts seconds from 1 January 1970 UTC. The Julian day counts days from noon on 1 January 4713 BC.

unix = seconds since 1970-01-01T00:00Z
JD = unix ÷ 86,400 + 2,440,587.5

Zulu notation

Write the UTC time as four digits and add Z.

14:30 UTC = 1430Z

Frequently asked questions

What is UTC?expand_more

Coordinated Universal Time, the world's time standard. It does not change for daylight saving. GMT is the same clock in practice.

What does Zulu mean?expand_more

Zulu is the military and aviation name for UTC. The letter Z stands for zero offset, so 1430Z is 14:30 UTC.

Is daylight saving handled?expand_more

Yes. Each zone's offset is worked out for the date you enter, so New York is UTC−4 in summer and UTC−5 in winter.

What is Unix time?expand_more

The number of seconds since 1 January 1970 at 00:00 UTC. It is the same everywhere.

Why does the day change in some cities?expand_more

A time near midnight UTC can already be the next day in zones ahead of UTC, such as Tokyo or Sydney.
