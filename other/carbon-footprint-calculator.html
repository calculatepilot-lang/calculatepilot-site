Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

local_gas_station

Fuel emissions

Burning a US gallon of gasoline releases 8,887 g of CO₂ and a gallon of diesel 10,180 g (EPA). Divide distance by fuel economy for the gallons used.

CO₂ = (distance ÷ mpg) × 8,887 g
bolt

Electric car emissions

Electricity used, plus about 5% lost in transmission, times the grid's carbon intensity.

CO₂ = distance × kWh/mi × 1.05 × g per kWh
park

Trees and offsets

The EPA counts 0.060 tonnes of CO₂ absorbed by one urban tree seedling grown for 10 years.

seedlings = tonnes ÷ 0.060
cost = tonnes × price per tonne

Worked examples

Illustrative figures, each checked by hand. The first is the default setup.

Midsize SUV

12,000 miles a year at 26 mpg (the default).

12,000 ÷ 26 = 461.5 gallons
461.5 × 8,887 g = 4.10 tonnes CO₂
= 342 g per mile, about 68 tree seedlings

Switching to electric

Same 12,000 miles, 0.30 kWh per mile, US average grid.

12,000 × 0.30 × 1.05 = 3,780 kWh
× 386 g = 1.46 tonnes, a saving of about 2.6 tonnes (64%)

One road trip

500 miles in a 30 mpg car.

500 ÷ 30 = 16.7 gallons
16.7 × 8,887 g = 148 kg CO₂

How to calculate your driving emissions

  • Choose units and whether the distance is a year or one trip.
  • Pick the vehicle type and enter its fuel economy or electricity use.
  • For an electric car, set the grid's carbon intensity.
  • Read the emissions, the comparison with other vehicles and the offset cost.

These are estimates from standard emission factors. They count the tailpipe (or the power plant) but not vehicle manufacturing or fuel production. Real fuel economy varies with driving style, load and temperature.

Frequently asked questions

How much CO₂ does a car emit per mile?expand_more

A gasoline car at 26 mpg emits about 342 g of CO₂ per mile. Each US gallon of gasoline burned releases 8,887 g of CO₂.

Are electric cars really cleaner?expand_more

Usually, even on an average grid, because power plants are more efficient than engines and grids keep getting cleaner. On a very coal-heavy grid the gap narrows.

How many trees offset a car?expand_more

By the EPA equivalency, one urban tree seedling grown for 10 years absorbs 0.060 tonnes of CO₂, so a typical car needs about 68 seedlings for a year of driving.

Does this include manufacturing?expand_more

No. It covers running emissions only.

Why does the offset price vary?expand_more

Offset prices depend on the project type and quality. The default of $15 per tonne is only a placeholder. Enter the price you are quoted.
