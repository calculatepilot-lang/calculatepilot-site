Moving rules worth knowing

Interstate moves in the United States are regulated by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA).

Estimates

A binding estimate fixes the price for the listed goods and services. A binding not-to-exceed estimate caps the price. A non-binding estimate can rise, but movers must limit what they can demand at delivery.

Weight and distance

Long-distance moves are priced by the weight of your goods and the distance, plus services. That is why weighing and decluttering before the move saves money.

Liability

Basic released value protection pays only 60 cents per pound per item, so a 10 lb item is covered for $6. Full value protection costs extra but pays to repair or replace.

Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

Full-service movers

Weight in hundredweight times a handling rate plus a distance rate, adjusted for season, plus services.

cost = (lb ÷ 100) × (handling + rate × miles ÷ 100) × season + extras
deployed_code

Moving container

Number of containers times a fixed fee plus a per-mile charge, adjusted for season. You pack and load.

cost = ⌈ cu ft ÷ size ⌉ × (fixed + per mile × miles) × season
directions_car

DIY rental truck

Truck base and mileage, fuel, lodging, tolls and helpers.

cost = base + per mile × miles + miles ÷ mpg × fuel + lodging + tolls + helpers

How to estimate the cost of a move

  • Choose the driving distance and the size of the move, then check the weight of your belongings.
  • Pick the season and any extra services such as packing or crating.
  • Edit the planning rates to match quotes you have received.
  • Compare movers, a container and a rental truck, and get written quotes before deciding.

These estimates are for planning only. Real prices vary widely by company, route, date and access, and long-distance movers give binding or non-binding estimates after a survey. Always get at least three written quotes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to move?expand_more

It varies greatly. Local moves are usually priced by the hour, while long-distance moves are priced by weight and distance. Use this tool for a planning figure, then get written quotes.

Is a container or a truck cheaper?expand_more

For long distances, a rental truck is usually the cheapest but you do the driving and lifting. Containers fall in the middle, and full-service movers cost the most but do the work. The comparison panel uses your assumptions.

How is a long-distance move priced?expand_more

Interstate moves are priced by the weight of your goods and the distance, plus services such as packing. Movers weigh the truck before and after loading.

What is released value protection?expand_more

It is the basic, no-extra-cost liability. It pays 60 cents per pound per item, so it does not cover the true value of most belongings. Full value protection costs extra but pays to repair or replace.

How can I lower my moving costs?expand_more

Declutter before you move, move in the off-season and mid-month, pack yourself, and compare quotes. Less weight and fewer services directly reduce a long-distance bill.
