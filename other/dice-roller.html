Dice Roller & Probability Calculator

Roll any dice expression such as 4d6kh3+2, see each die, and get the exact probability of every total. It supports keep and drop rules, modifiers, exploding dice and a 10,000-roll simulation.

How the engine works

Rolls use the browser's secure random generator with rejection sampling. The odds are counted exactly for plain sums and for keep or drop rules on small pools.

Why we built it

Tabletop players want a quick roll and also want to know how likely a result was. This gives both together.

Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

functions

Mean and variance

For one die with s sides, then added up for n dice.

mean = n × (s + 1) ÷ 2
variance = n × (s² − 1) ÷ 12
bar_chart

Exact probability of a total

Counted by combining the dice one at a time. With keep or drop rules, every combination is counted when there are few, otherwise the chart is simulated.

P(total = k) = ways to make k ÷ sn
shuffle

Fair random rolls

Rolls use the browser's secure random generator. Values that would bias the result are discarded and redrawn.

roll = (random mod s) + 1, redraw if in the biased tail

8d6.

How to use the dice roller

  • Type an expression such as 2d6+3, or tap dice and presets to build one.
  • Roll the dice. Dropped dice are shown struck through.
  • Read the expected value, chance of this result, and the probability chart.
  • Run 10,000 rolls to compare the simulation with the exact odds.

Rolls are random. Past results do not change future odds.

Frequently asked questions

What does 4d6kh3 mean?expand_more

Roll four six-sided dice and keep the highest three. It is a common way to generate character ability scores, giving an average of about 12.24.

What is advantage and disadvantage?expand_more

Advantage is rolling two d20 and keeping the higher (2d20kh1). Disadvantage keeps the lower (2d20kl1).

Are the rolls truly random?expand_more

They come from the browser's cryptographic random source, which is suitable for games. Values that would cause modulo bias are discarded.

What are exploding dice?expand_more

When a die shows its highest number you roll it again and add the result, repeating while it keeps showing the maximum.

Why do sums cluster in the middle?expand_more

There are more ways to make middle totals. With 2d6, seven can be made six ways while two can be made only one way.
