Common height conversion reference points

A few frequently referenced conversions: 5'0" equals about 152 cm, 5'6" equals about 168 cm, 6'0" equals about 183 cm. These rough benchmarks can be useful for quick mental estimates before using the precise calculator above for exact figures.

Height conversion frequently comes up when filling out international forms, comparing measurements across countries that use different systems, or discussing height in a context using an unfamiliar unit system.

A worked example

5 feet 8 inches converts to 68 total inches, or 172.7 cm (68 times 2.54), which is 1.727 meters.

6 feet 2 inches converts to 74 inches, or 188.0 cm — a common reference height that illustrates how each additional inch adds exactly 2.54 cm regardless of the starting height.

Frequently asked questions

How is feet and inches converted to centimeters?

Total height in inches is multiplied by 2.54 (the number of centimeters in one inch).

How precise is the centimeter conversion?

This calculator uses the exact conversion factor (1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly), so the result is as precise as your feet/inches input allows.

Why do height conversions sometimes round differently on different sites?

Different rounding conventions (nearest whole centimeter vs. one decimal place) can produce slightly different displayed results even from the same underlying precise conversion.

How many centimeters is 6 feet?

6 feet converts to approximately 182.9 centimeters (72 inches × 2.54 cm/inch).

What is 5 feet 7 inches in cm?

5 feet 7 inches is 170.18 cm. The formula: multiply feet by 30.48 and add inches multiplied by 2.54. So (5 × 30.48) + (7 × 2.54) = 152.4 + 17.78 = 170.18 cm.

How do I convert height from feet to cm?

Multiply the number of feet by 30.48 to get centimetres. For mixed feet and inches, multiply the feet by 30.48 and the inches by 2.54, then add them together. For example, 5'10" = (5 × 30.48) + (10 × 2.54) = 177.8 cm.

What is 5'5 in cm?

5 feet 5 inches = 165.1 cm. Calculation: (5 × 30.48) + (5 × 2.54) = 152.4 + 12.7 = 165.1 cm.

What is 5'7 in cm?

5 feet 7 inches = 170.18 cm. Calculation: (5 × 30.48) + (7 × 2.54) = 152.4 + 17.78 = 170.18 cm.

What is 5'10 in cm?

5 feet 10 inches = 177.8 cm. Calculation: (5 × 30.48) + (10 × 2.54) = 152.4 + 25.4 = 177.8 cm.

What is 6 feet in cm?

6 feet = 182.88 cm. Calculation: 6 × 30.48 = 182.88 cm.

How do you convert feet and inches to cm?

Multiply the feet by 30.48, then multiply the inches by 2.54, then add both results. Formula: cm = (feet × 30.48) + (inches × 2.54).
