Formulas

Area formulas used

Rectangle

A = L × W

Triangle

A = ½ × base × height

Circle

A = π × (diameter ÷ 2)²

Feet and inches convert to decimal feet as ft + in ÷ 12. One square yard is 9 sq ft, one acre is 43,560 sq ft, and one square foot is 0.09290304 m².

Tips

Measuring a room

Measure wall to wall at the widest points. For an L-shaped room, split it into rectangles, add each to the ledger and read the total.

Leave out fixed cabinets or islands only if you will not floor under them. Add the waste margin last, not before.

How to calculate square footage

  • Measure the length and width of the space in feet, or in feet and inches.
  • Multiply length by width for a rectangle. Use the triangle or circle formula for other shapes.
  • Split irregular rooms into simple shapes and add their areas.
  • Add a waste margin before buying flooring, tile or other materials.

Square footage for listings can follow different rules than this simple floor area. For sales or rent, check the measurement standard your market uses.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square feet from feet and inches?expand_more

Convert each side to decimal feet by dividing the inches by 12 and adding them to the feet, then multiply. A 12 ft 6 in by 9 ft 3 in room is 12.5 × 9.25 = 115.63 square feet.

How much waste should I add for flooring?expand_more

Five to 10% covers a simple rectangular room. Use about 15% for diagonal layouts, herringbone, or rooms with many angles or doorways.

What is the difference between square feet and linear feet?expand_more

Square feet measure area, length times width. Linear feet measure length only, for things such as baseboards or fencing. The Perimeter option shows the linear feet around a rectangular room.

How do I measure an L-shaped room?expand_more

Split the room into two rectangles, calculate each and add them. In this tool, add each rectangle to the ledger and read the total.

How many square feet are in a square yard?expand_more

There are 9 square feet in a square yard, because a yard is 3 feet and 3 × 3 = 9. Carpet is often sold by the square yard.
