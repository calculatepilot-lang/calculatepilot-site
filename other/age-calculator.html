How age is calculated here

This calculator counts complete years, then complete months within the current year, then remaining days — the same way age is conventionally stated, rather than just dividing total days by 365.

Why exact age calculations can be tricky

Calculating exact age requires handling varying month lengths and leap years. Manual date subtraction often produces off-by-one errors. This calculator handles those edge cases automatically.

A worked example

Someone born March 15, 1995, as of July 24, 2026 is 31 years, 4 months, and 9 days — about 11,464 days lived, with the next birthday about 234 days away.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't my age in days match years × 365?

Leap years add extra days roughly every four years, so total days lived is always slightly higher than years × 365.

Can I calculate age as of a future or past date?

Yes — change the Age at date field to any date to see age as of that point.

Why is current year minus birth year sometimes wrong?

If the birthday has not occurred yet this year, that subtraction overstates age by one year.
