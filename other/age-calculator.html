How age is calculated here

This calculator counts complete years first, then complete months, then leftover days. That matches how people usually state age (“31 years, 4 months, and 9 days”) rather than a raw day count alone.

Need the number of days between two arbitrary dates instead of an age breakdown? Use the Date Calculator.

A worked example

Born 15 March 1995, measured on 24 July 2026:

  • Complete years: 31 (15 March 2026)
  • Complete months after that: 4 (to 15 July 2026)
  • Remaining days: 9
  • Result: 31 years, 4 months, and 9 days

Leap years and birthday edge cases

Leap days are included because the tool uses real calendar dates. If someone was born on 29 February, most systems treat 1 March as the birthday in non-leap years. Total days lived will be higher than “years × 365” because leap days add extra days over time.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate exact age?

Count complete years from the birth date to the target date, then complete months, then remaining days.

Does this calculator include leap years?

Yes. Age is computed from real calendar dates, so 29 February and other leap days are counted correctly.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the Age at date field. That is useful for a school cutoff, visa form, or future birthday.

Why do websites show different ages in days?

Some tools ignore leap days or use 365-day years. This page uses the actual calendar, so the day count can differ by a day or two.
