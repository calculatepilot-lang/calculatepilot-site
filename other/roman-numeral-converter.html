Roman numeral reference

Seven letters give every number: I 1, V 5, X 10, L 50, C 100, D 500, M 1,000. A smaller letter before a larger one is subtracted. A bar over a numeral multiplies it by 1,000.

Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

123

Number to Roman

Take the largest numeral value that fits, write it down, subtract, and repeat.

2026 = 1000 + 1000 + 10 + 10 + 5 + 1 = MMXXVI
swap_horiz

Roman to number

Add each value, but subtract a letter that stands before a bigger one.

MCMXCIV = 1000 + (1000 − 100) + (100 − 10) + (5 − 1) = 1994
looks_one

Large numbers

A bar (vinculum) over letters multiplies them by 1,000. In brackets, (V) also means 5,000.

(V)DC = 5,000 + 600 = 5,600

Frequently asked questions

How do you write 4 in Roman numerals?expand_more

IV in standard notation. IIII (additive) is also seen, especially on clock faces.

What is the largest Roman numeral?expand_more

Classical numerals stop at 3,999 (MMMCMXCIX). A bar over a numeral multiplies it by 1,000, which here extends the range to 3,999,999.

Is there a zero in Roman numerals?expand_more

No. The Romans had no symbol for zero, though they had the word nulla.

Why is IIII on clocks?expand_more

It balances the face visually against VIII, and it has a long tradition. Both IV and IIII are historically valid.

How do I write a date in Roman numerals?expand_more

Convert the day, month and year each to numerals. 24 October 2026 becomes XXIV.X.MMXXVI.
