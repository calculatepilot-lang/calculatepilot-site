MPG, L/100 km and km/L

US MPG converts to metric fuel economy with L/100 km = 235.215 ÷ MPG. Lower L/100 km is better, while higher MPG and km/L are better.

Formulas

How the numbers are calculated

Fuel used and cost

gallons = miles ÷ MPG
cost = gallons × price

Measured MPG

MPG = (odometer₂ − odometer₁) ÷ gallons

Emissions and EV

CO₂ = gallons × 8.887 kg
EV cost = miles ÷ (mi/kWh) × price

One US gallon is 3.785412 litres and one mile is 1.609344 km. Burning a gallon of gasoline emits about 8.887 kg of CO₂ (US EPA).

Real-world MPG

Why yours may differ

Speed, acceleration, tire pressure, cold weather, cargo, air conditioning and short trips all lower fuel economy. Highway cruising at moderate speed is usually the most efficient.

Tracking several fill-ups gives a better average than a single one.

Worked examples

Illustrative trips, each checked by hand. The first is the default setup.

340-mile trip

28.5 MPG, gas at $3.55 a gallon.

340 ÷ 28.5 = 11.93 gal
11.93 × 3.55 = $42.35
CO₂ = 11.93 × 8.887 = 106.0 kg
EV: 340 ÷ 3.5 = 97.1 kWh × 0.16 = $15.54

MPG from a fill-up

Odometer 48,250 to 48,590, and 11.93 gallons added.

340 miles ÷ 11.93 gal = 28.5 MPG
235.215 ÷ 28.5 = 8.25 L/100 km
cost per mile = 3.55 ÷ 28.5 = $0.125

Splitting a road trip

340 miles each way, four people.

round trip = 680 mi
680 ÷ 28.5 × 3.55 = $84.70
÷ 4 = $21.18 each

How to calculate gas mileage

  • Fill the tank and note the odometer, or reset the trip meter.
  • Drive as normal, then fill up again and record the gallons added.
  • Divide the miles driven by the gallons added to get miles per gallon.
  • Repeat a few times and average the results for a reliable figure.

To budget a trip, divide its distance by your MPG for gallons, then multiply by the price of gas along the route.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate miles per gallon?expand_more

Divide the miles you drove by the gallons of fuel you used. If you drove 340 miles and added 11.93 gallons at the next full fill-up, your fuel economy is 340 ÷ 11.93 = 28.5 MPG.

How do I convert MPG to L/100 km?expand_more

Divide 235.215 by the US MPG figure. For example, 28.5 MPG is 8.25 L/100 km. The same formula converts back: MPG = 235.215 ÷ L/100 km. UK gallons are larger, so UK MPG uses 282.481.

How is the carbon emission worked out?expand_more

The EPA gives 8,887 grams (8.887 kg) of CO₂ for each gallon of gasoline burned. Multiply gallons used by that factor for the tailpipe CO₂ of the trip. It does not include emissions from producing the fuel.

How does the EV comparison work?expand_more

Enter miles per kWh (many EVs manage 3 to 4) and your electricity price. Trip miles divided by miles per kWh gives kWh used, and multiplying by the price gives the charging cost. Public fast chargers cost more than home charging.

Why is my MPG lower in winter?expand_more

Cold engines run richer, cold air is denser, tires lose pressure, and winter fuel blends and heating use energy. Short trips make it worse because the engine spends more time warming up.
