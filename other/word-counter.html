Formulas

Readability equations

Flesch Reading Ease

206.835 − 1.015 × (words ÷ sentences) − 84.6 × (syllables ÷ words)

Flesch-Kincaid grade

0.39 × (words ÷ sentences) + 11.8 × (syllables ÷ words) − 15.59

Coleman-Liau and ARI

CLI = 0.0588 L − 0.296 S − 15.8
ARI = 4.71 × (letters ÷ words) + 0.5 × (words ÷ sentences) − 21.43

Gunning Fog is 0.4 × ((words ÷ sentences) + 100 × (words of 3+ syllables ÷ words)). L is letters and S is sentences per 100 words.

How to use a word counter

  • Paste your text into the box. Counts and scores update as you type.
  • Check words and characters against your assignment, post or snippet limit.
  • Use the readability scores to see whether shorter sentences and simpler words would help.
  • Look at the keyword table to spot words you repeat too often.

Word and sentence counts are estimates. Abbreviations, numbers and unusual punctuation can change how sentences and syllables are counted, and readability scores suit English prose only.

Frequently asked questions

How does the tool count words?expand_more

It counts groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks, ignoring stray symbols. Hyphenated words such as "well-known" count as one word, and numbers count as words.

What is a good Flesch Reading Ease score?expand_more

For general audiences, 60 to 70 is plain English that most adults find easy. Web content and marketing often aim for 60 or higher, while technical and academic writing can score much lower.

How is reading time worked out?expand_more

Word count divided by reading speed. The default is 238 words per minute, the average for adult silent reading of non-fiction, and speaking time uses 150 words per minute.

Is my text sent to a server?expand_more

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded or saved. The share link contains only your settings, not your text.

Why do readability tools disagree?expand_more

Each formula weighs sentence length, syllables or letters differently, and syllable counting is an estimate. Treat the scores as a guide and look at the range, not a single number.
