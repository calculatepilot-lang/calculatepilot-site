Cats: indoor and outdoor lifespans

The aging rate of a cat is the same on paper, but the years a cat gets to live are not. Lifestyle and health care matter greatly, and figures below are broad ranges.

Indoor only

Commonly 12 to 18 years or more, equal to roughly 64 to 88 human years. Lower risk of trauma, infectious disease and toxins.

Indoor and outdoor

Often shorter, since traffic, fights, parasites and infections add risk.

Outdoor or feral

Life expectancy is often far lower because of weather, hunger, injury and untreated disease.

Methods

How the ages are estimated

Epigenetic clock (dogs)

human = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31

Size-based table (dogs)

first year 15, second 24, then a yearly rate that grows with size

Cats

year 1 = 15, year 2 = 24, then +4 per year
Why not ×7?

The 7-year rule is a myth

A one-year-old dog is closer to a mid-teen person than a 7-year-old child, and large dogs age faster in later life than small ones. The formulas here reflect that.

No conversion is exact. Genetics, diet, exercise and health care all change how an individual pet ages.

How to convert dog and cat years to human years

  • Choose dog or cat, and for a dog pick its size by adult weight.
  • Enter the age in years and months.
  • Read the equivalent human age and the life stage.
  • Use the life stage to plan checkups, dental care and senior screening with your vet.

The two dog methods give different answers, especially in older dogs. Treat both as rough guides and rely on your vet's judgement for your own pet.

Frequently asked questions

How old is my dog in human years?expand_more

It depends on age and size. A medium dog aged 5 is about 36 human years by the veterinary table, and the epigenetic formula gives a higher figure for the same age. Enter your dog's details above.

Why do large dogs age faster?expand_more

Larger breeds grow quickly and tend to have shorter lifespans, so each later calendar year represents more biological aging than for a small dog. Giant breeds often reach senior status by 6 to 7 years.

How do I calculate a cat's age in human years?expand_more

The common guideline counts the first year as 15, the second year as 24, and then adds 4 for each year after that. A 10-year-old cat is about 56 human years.

What is the epigenetic clock formula?expand_more

Researchers at the University of California San Diego compared DNA methylation in dogs and humans and found human age ≈ 16 × ln(dog age) + 31. It was developed from Labrador retrievers, so it may not fit every breed.

When is a pet a senior?expand_more

Cats are considered mature at 7 to 10 years and senior from about 11. Dogs become senior earlier the larger they are, from roughly 6 to 7 for giant breeds to 10 or more for small dogs.
