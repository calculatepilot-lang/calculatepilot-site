How this calculator works

GPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points. Leave credits at 0 for unused rows.

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) / Σ(credits)

A 3-credit A (4.0) contributes 12 quality points. A 4-credit B (3.0) contributes 12 quality points. The two courses together are 7 credits and 24 quality points, so GPA = 24 ÷ 7 ≈ 3.43.

A worked example

Four 3-credit courses graded A, A−, B+, and B:

  • Quality points = (4.0×3) + (3.7×3) + (3.3×3) + (3.0×3) = 42.0
  • Credits = 12
  • GPA = 42.0 ÷ 12 = 3.50

Frequently asked questions

How is GPA calculated on a 4.0 scale?

Multiply each course grade point by its credit hours, add those quality points, then divide by total credit hours.

Do credit hours matter?

Yes. A 4-credit A raises GPA more than a 1-credit A. Always weight by credits.

What grade points does this calculator use?

A 4.0, A− 3.7, B+ 3.3, B 3.0, B− 2.7, C+ 2.3, C 2.0, D 1.0, F 0.0. Some schools use slightly different plus/minus maps.

Can I leave a course blank?

Set unused rows to 0 credits. Those rows are ignored.
