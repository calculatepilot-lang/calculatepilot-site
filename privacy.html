Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CalculatePilot ('we', 'our', 'the site') handles information when you use our calculators. We built this site to require no account, no login, and no submission of personal data to use any calculator — all calculations run locally in your browser.

Information we collect

CalculatePilot does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use any calculator. The values you enter into a calculator (your income, weight, loan amount, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Like most websites, we may use standard analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns — such as which pages are visited and general geographic or device information — to help us improve the site. This data is typically anonymized or aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for basic site functionality and analytics purposes. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some site functionality but will not prevent you from using our calculators.

Third-party services

If this site displays advertising or affiliate links in the future, those third-party providers may use their own cookies or tracking technologies subject to their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review separately.

Children's privacy

CalculatePilot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the information provided on our website.
