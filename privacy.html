Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CalculatePilot ('we', 'our', 'the site') handles information when you use our calculators. You do not need an account or login to use any calculator, and calculator inputs are processed in your browser rather than submitted to our servers for storage.

Analytics and consent

CalculatePilot uses optional analytics services to understand aggregate site usage and improve the website. Google Analytics and Ahrefs Analytics are third-party analytics services. They are not loaded until you explicitly choose Allow analytics in our consent banner. If you reject analytics, those services are not loaded.

You can change your analytics choice at any time using the Privacy settings control displayed on the site after your initial choice.

Information we collect

CalculatePilot does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use any calculator. The values you enter into a calculator (for example, income, weight, loan amount, or other calculation inputs) are processed entirely in your browser and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers by the calculator itself.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your analytics consent preference in browser local storage so that we can remember whether you allowed or rejected optional analytics. This preference is necessary to honor your choice. Optional analytics technologies are activated only after you grant analytics consent.

Third-party services

When you allow analytics, Google Analytics and Ahrefs Analytics may process information about your visit according to their respective terms and privacy policies. We do not intentionally send calculator input values to these analytics services.

Children's privacy

CalculatePilot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the information provided on our website.
