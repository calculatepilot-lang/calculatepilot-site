Section 01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CalculatePilot ('we', 'our', 'the site') handles information when you use our calculators. You do not need an account or login to use any calculator, and calculator inputs are processed in your browser rather than submitted to our servers for storage.

lock

Calculated in Your Browser

Whether you are estimating a mortgage schedule, a BMR or an algebra problem, the values you type are processed on your own device.

Section 02

Analytics and consent

CalculatePilot uses optional analytics services to understand aggregate site usage and improve the website. Google Analytics, Ahrefs Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are third-party analytics services. They are not loaded until you explicitly choose Allow analytics in our consent banner. If you reject analytics, those services are not loaded.

You can change your analytics choice at any time using the Privacy settings control displayed on the site after your initial choice.

shield Off Until You Agree

Analytics scripts are not loaded until you choose Allow analytics.

history Change Your Mind Any Time

Use the Privacy settings control to change your analytics choice at any time.

Section 03

Information we collect

CalculatePilot does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use any calculator. The values you enter into a calculator (for example, income, weight, loan amount, or other calculation inputs) are processed entirely in your browser and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers by the calculator itself.

Data Type Storage Location Transmitted to Server?
Financial Inputs (Salary, Loan, Debt) Local Device RAM close Never
Health Inputs (Weight, Height, Age) Local Device RAM close Never
User Accounts / Personal Passwords Non-existent close None
Consent Preference Browser LocalStorage close Client Only
Section 04

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your analytics consent preference in browser local storage so that we can remember whether you allowed or rejected optional analytics. This preference is necessary to honor your choice. Optional analytics technologies are activated only after you grant analytics consent.

Section 05

Third-party services

When you allow analytics, Google Analytics, Ahrefs Analytics and Microsoft Clarity may process information about your visit according to their respective terms and privacy policies. We do not intentionally send calculator input values to these analytics services.
