    What is area?

    Area measures the amount of flat surface inside a boundary, in square units: a 3 m by 4 m rectangle covers 12 m², meaning twelve 1 m by 1 m squares. Perimeter, by contrast, is the length of the boundary.

    Two formulas worth knowing

    • Heron’s formula gives a triangle’s area from its three sides alone: A = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)], where s is half the perimeter.
    • The Shoelace formula gives the area of any simple polygon from its ordered vertices: A = ½|Σ(xᵢyᵢ₊₁ − xᵢ₊₁yᵢ)|. Surveyors use it for parcels defined by coordinates.

    Area of an L-shape or any composite shape

    Split the shape into rectangles and triangles, find each area, then add them. Or measure the bounding rectangle and subtract the missing part. Example: an L-shaped room is 6 m by 4 m with a 2 m by 2 m corner missing.

    • Add: 6 × 2 + 4 × 2 = 12 + 8 = 20 m².
    • Subtract: 6 × 4 − 2 × 2 = 24 − 4 = 20 m².
    • Perimeter: with the notch in a corner it equals the bounding rectangle, 2(6 + 4) = 20 m.

    If you know the corner coordinates, choose Irregular polygon and enter them instead.

    Square feet and square metres

    1 ft = 0.3048 m exactly, so 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m² and 1 m² ≈ 10.7639 ft². To convert ft² to m² multiply by 0.0929; to go the other way multiply by 10.764. An acre is 43,560 ft².

    Measuring tips

    • Measure every length in the same unit before you calculate.
    • For a room, measure the longest and shortest wall separately in case it is not square.
    • Use perpendicular height for triangles, parallelograms and trapezoids, not the slanted side.
    • A diameter is twice the radius; halve it before using the circle formula.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is the area of a circle πr² and not something with the diameter?expand_more

    Area scales with the square of length, and the circle’s size is set by one length, the radius. Archimedes showed that a circle has the same area as a right triangle whose legs are the radius and the circumference: ½ × r × 2πr = πr². In terms of the diameter it is π d² ÷ 4.

    How does the Shoelace formula work, and does vertex order matter?expand_more

    It sums the cross products of consecutive vertex coordinates around the boundary. Vertices must be listed in order around the edge (clockwise or anticlockwise); the absolute value handles either direction. If edges cross each other, the formula no longer gives the enclosed area.

    How do I convert between square metres, square feet and acres?expand_more

    Convert the length factor and square it: 1 ft = 0.3048 m, so 1 ft² = 0.09290304 m², and 1 m² ≈ 10.7639 ft². An acre is 4,046.8564224 m² (43,560 international ft²), and a hectare is 10,000 m². The table above does all of these at once.

    Is the ellipse perimeter exact?expand_more

    The area πab is exact. The perimeter of an ellipse has no simple closed form, so this calculator uses Ramanujan’s second approximation, which is accurate to well under one part in a million for ordinary ellipses.

    Why do trapezoid and parallelogram perimeters need extra sides?expand_more

    Their areas depend only on the base(s) and the perpendicular height, but the slanted sides can have different lengths for the same height, so the perimeter cannot be found from those inputs alone.

    Which units should I use for the inputs?expand_more

    Any length unit, as long as every dimension uses the same one. The area comes out in that unit squared, and the conversion table shows it in other area units.

    How do I find the area of an L-shaped room?expand_more

    Split it into two rectangles and add them, or take the bounding rectangle and subtract the missing corner. A 6 m by 4 m rectangle with a 2 m by 2 m corner missing has 20 m² either way.

    Can I get area from perimeter?expand_more

    No. Shapes with the same perimeter can have different areas: a 12 m perimeter gives 5, 8 or 9 m² for rectangles of 5 by 1, 4 by 2 and 3 by 3.

    How do I calculate the area of a rhombus or kite?expand_more

    Multiply the two diagonals and halve the result. Diagonals of 8 and 6 give 24. You can also enter the four corners under Irregular polygon.

    How do I convert square feet to square metres?expand_more

    Multiply by 0.0929. Going the other way, multiply square metres by 10.764.

    How do I find the area of a semicircle?expand_more

    Calculate the full circle with pi times r squared and halve it. A radius of 5 gives 39.27.
