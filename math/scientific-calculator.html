    Frequently asked questions

    Why does 2 + 3 × 4 equal 14 here, not 20?expand_more

    This calculator evaluates full algebraic expressions using standard order of operations (PEMDAS): multiplication before addition, so 3×4=12 is computed first, then 2+12=14. Our Basic Calculator instead applies operators immediately left to right, like a simple handheld calculator.

    Why does 2^3^2 equal 512, not 64?expand_more

    Exponentiation is right-associative: 2^3^2 means 2^(3^2) = 2^9 = 512, not (2^3)^2 = 64. This matches standard mathematical convention.

    What's the difference between DEG and RAD mode?expand_more

    It controls the units trig functions expect (and return, for inverse functions). sin(30) in degree mode is 0.5; in radian mode, 30 is interpreted as 30 radians instead, giving a very different result.

    Is this safe — does it use eval()?expand_more

    No. It uses a hand-written recursive-descent parser that only recognizes numbers, named functions and arithmetic operators — it cannot execute arbitrary code, unlike JavaScript's eval().

    What does log vs. ln mean?expand_more

    log is base-10 logarithm (log₁₀); ln is the natural logarithm (loge, base e ≈ 2.71828). Use our Log Calculator for any other base.
