    Key identities

    Pythagorean

    sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
    1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
    1 + cot²θ = csc²θ

    Follow from x² + y² = 1 on the unit circle.

    Double angle

    sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ
    cos 2θ = cos²θ − sin²θ
    tan 2θ = 2 tan θ ÷ (1 − tan²θ)

    Euler’s formula

    e^(iθ) = cos θ + i sin θ
    cosh x = (eˣ + e⁻ˣ) ÷ 2
    sinh x = (eˣ − e⁻ˣ) ÷ 2

    What are the trigonometric functions?

    For an angle θ in a right triangle, sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse and tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent. Their reciprocals are csc θ = 1/sin θ, sec θ = 1/cos θ and cot θ = 1/tan θ. On the unit circle, cos θ and sin θ are the x and y coordinates of the point at angle θ, which extends the definitions to all angles.

    Degrees, radians and gradians

    A full turn is 360° = 2π rad = 400 grad. Convert with rad = deg × π ÷ 180 and grad = deg × 10 ÷ 9. Calculus and most science use radians because derivatives such as d/dx sin x = cos x hold only in radians.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why use radians instead of degrees in calculus?expand_more

    Radian measure is the ratio of arc length to radius, so it needs no arbitrary scale factor. The key limit lim sin x ÷ x = 1 holds only in radians, and therefore d/dx sin x = cos x. In degrees an extra factor of π/180 appears in every derivative.

    Why is tan 90° undefined?expand_more

    tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ, and cos 90° = 0, so the quotient divides by zero. The tangent function has vertical asymptotes at odd multiples of 90°, where secant is also undefined.

    What are the ranges of the inverse functions?expand_more

    The principal values are: arcsin in −90° to 90°, arccos in 0° to 180°, arctan in −90° to 90° (exclusive), arccot in 0° to 180° (exclusive). Because trig functions repeat, other angles share the same value; the Inverse tab lists them within one full turn.

    What is a reference angle?expand_more

    It is the acute angle between the terminal side and the x-axis. Every trig value of θ equals the value for its reference angle up to a sign determined by the quadrant (All-Sine-Tangent-Cosine: all positive in I, sine in II, tangent in III, cosine in IV).

    How accurate is the result?expand_more

    The functions are evaluated in IEEE 754 double precision, about 15 to 16 significant digits. Values that are mathematically zero (such as cos 90°) are cleaned to exactly 0. Exact radical forms are shown for angles that are multiples of 30° or 45°.

    What are hyperbolic functions?expand_more

    sinh, cosh and tanh are built from exponentials: sinh x = (eˣ − e⁻ˣ)/2 and cosh x = (eˣ + e⁻ˣ)/2. They satisfy cosh²x − sinh²x = 1 and describe hanging cables (catenaries) and relativistic rapidity.
