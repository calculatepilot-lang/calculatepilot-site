Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about licensing, embedding and billing.

How do I receive my Pro license key after payment? expand_more
Your license key is generated and emailed to you automatically as soon as your payment is confirmed through Creem. Enter it on the embed page at calculatepilot.com/embed/ and your embed codes update to the white-label version.
Does a single Pro license cover multiple websites or client projects? expand_more
Yes. One Pro license covers unlimited domains, so you can embed white-label calculators on as many sites as you need.
What happens to my embedded calculators if I cancel my subscription? expand_more
Your embeds keep working. They revert to the Free plan automatically and show the CalculatePilot branding again.
What payment methods are supported? expand_more
All major debit and credit cards, processed securely through Creem.
Can I test the embeds before buying Pro? expand_more
Yes. The Free plan lets you embed any of the 159 calculators immediately, with no account, at /embed/. Pick a calculator, copy the snippet and paste it into your site.
