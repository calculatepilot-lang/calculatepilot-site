Stage 1 vs Stage 2 Hypertension: Blood Pressure Numbers Compared

The two hypertension stages side by side, with example readings and the cut-offs that trip people up.

→ Use the free Blood Pressure Category Calculator

Short answer: Stage 1 hypertension is a systolic of 130–139 or a diastolic of 80–89. Stage 2 is a systolic of 140 or higher or a diastolic of 90 or higher. Either number alone is enough to put you in the higher stage.

Where stage 2 ends

A reading higher than 180 systolic and/or higher than 120 diastolic is classed as severe hypertension. Recheck after a minute, and follow the steps in the hypertensive crisis guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is stage 1 hypertension?

A systolic of 130–139 or a diastolic of 80–89.

What is stage 2 hypertension?

A systolic of 140 or higher or a diastolic of 90 or higher.

Is 140/90 stage 1 or stage 2?

Stage 2, because a systolic of 140 or a diastolic of 90 is enough.

Is 180/110 a hypertensive crisis?

Not by the numbers alone: 180 is not higher than 180, so it is stage 2. A systolic above 180 or a diastolic above 120 is severe.
