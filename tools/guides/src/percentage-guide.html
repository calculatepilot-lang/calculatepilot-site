How to Calculate Percentages — Complete Guide with Examples

Three types of percentage calculations explained with formulas and real examples. Use our free calculator for instant answers.

Three formulas:
1. X% of a number: Number × (X ÷ 100)
2. What % is A of B: (A ÷ B) × 100
3. % change: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Quick percentage shortcuts

  • 10% — divide by 10
  • 5% — find 10% then halve it
  • 20% — divide by 5
  • 25% — divide by 4
  • 50% — divide by 2

How do I add VAT (20%) to a price?

Multiply by 1.20. Example: £100 + 20% VAT = £100 × 1.20 = £120. To remove VAT from a gross price, divide by 1.20.

How do I calculate a percentage tip?

For 15%: find 10% (divide by 10), add half of that (5%). Example: 15% tip on $45 = $4.50 + $2.25 = $6.75. For 20%: divide by 5.
