Binary to Decimal to Hex Conversion Chart (0–15) With Examples

A 0 to 15 reference chart and the methods for binary to decimal, decimal to binary and binary to hex.

→ Use the free Binary Calculator

Short answer: Binary to decimal: add the place values of the 1 bits, so 101101 = 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45. Decimal to binary: divide by 2 and read the remainders upward. Binary to hex: group bits in fours, so 11111111 = FF.

Binary to decimal

Each position is a power of 2, from 1 on the right. 101101 = 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45.

Decimal to binary

Divide by 2 and keep the remainders: 45 → 22 r1, 22 → 11 r0, 11 → 5 r1, 5 → 2 r1, 2 → 1 r0, 1 → 0 r1. Read upward: 101101.

Binary to hex

Group the bits in fours from the right and use the chart. 101101 becomes 0010 1101, which is 2D. Check: 2 × 16 + 13 = 45.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert binary to decimal?

Add the place values (1, 2, 4, 8 and so on from the right) of every bit that is 1.

How do I convert decimal to binary?

Divide by 2 repeatedly and read the remainders from the bottom up.

How do I convert binary to hex?

Group the bits in fours from the right and replace each group with its hex digit.

What is 11111111 in decimal?

255, which is FF in hex.
