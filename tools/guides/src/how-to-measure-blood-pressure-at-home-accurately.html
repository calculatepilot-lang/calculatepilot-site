How to Measure Blood Pressure at Home Accurately (Step by Step)

A checklist for taking a reliable home reading and what to do with the numbers.

→ Use the free Blood Pressure Category Calculator

Short answer: Rest for 5 minutes, sit with your back supported, feet flat and legs uncrossed, rest your arm at heart level, avoid caffeine, exercise and smoking for 30 minutes first, and take two or more readings. Then average them.

Before you start

  • Avoid caffeine, exercise and smoking for 30 minutes.
  • Empty your bladder.
  • Sit quietly for 5 minutes.

Positioning

  1. Sit with your back supported and feet flat on the floor, legs uncrossed.
  2. Rest your arm on a table with the cuff at heart level.
  3. Place the cuff on bare skin, not over clothing, and use the right cuff size.
  4. Stay still and do not talk while it measures.

Take more than one reading

Take two or more readings and use the average. Example: 128/82, 132/84 and 126/80 average to 129/82, which is stage 1 hypertension because the diastolic is in the 80–89 band.

Common mistakes

MistakeEffect
No rest firstReading can be falsely high
Cuff over clothingReading can be inaccurate
Arm below heart levelReading can be falsely high
Talking or crossed legsReading can be falsely high

What next

Enter your readings in the Blood Pressure Category Calculator to average them and see the category. A single high reading is not a diagnosis; share the pattern of your readings with a clinician. Categories are in the blood pressure chart explained.

Frequently asked questions

How long should I rest before measuring?

Sit quietly for 5 minutes.

Should I take more than one reading?

Yes. Take two or more and use the average.

How long should I avoid caffeine before measuring?

At least 30 minutes, and avoid exercise and smoking as well.

Where should my arm be?

Resting on a support with the cuff at heart level.
