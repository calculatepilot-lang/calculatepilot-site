How Long Does It Take to Sober Up? BAC Time to Zero

How fast the body clears alcohol and what that means for planning, with the time to zero for common BAC levels.

→ Use the free BAC Calculator

Short answer: The body clears roughly 0.015% BAC per hour on average, so 0.08% takes about 5.3 hours to reach zero, 0.10% about 6.7 hours and 0.15% about 10 hours. Measured rates vary roughly from 0.009% to 0.035% per hour.

Time to zero

BACTime to 0.00%
0.04%2.7 hours
0.08%5.3 hours
0.10%6.7 hours
0.15%10.0 hours
0.20%13.3 hours

This uses 0.015% per hour, a commonly used conservative rate. Real elimination ranges roughly between 0.009% and 0.035% per hour from person to person.

What does not help

Coffee, cold showers and exercise do not speed up elimination. Only time lowers BAC.

Alcohol can still be absorbing

BAC can keep rising for a while after the last drink, so an estimate taken soon afterwards can be too low.

Planning

  • Arrange a sober ride before drinking.
  • Do not use the time to zero to decide when it is safe to drive.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to sober up?

About 5.3 hours from a 0.08% BAC at the average estimated rate of 0.015% per hour.

Does coffee help me sober up?

No. Only time lowers BAC.

Is the elimination rate the same for everyone?

No. Measured rates range roughly from 0.009% to 0.035% per hour.

Can I drive when the calculator says zero?

Do not rely on it. It is only an estimate.
