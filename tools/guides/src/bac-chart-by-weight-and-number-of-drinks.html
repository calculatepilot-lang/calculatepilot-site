BAC Chart by Weight and Number of Drinks (Men and Women)

Peak blood alcohol estimates by body weight, sex and number of standard drinks.

→ Use the free BAC Calculator

Short answer: Peak BAC after 1 to 5 standard drinks ranges from about 0.02% (200 lb man, 1 drink) to 0.23% (120 lb woman, 5 drinks) with no time elapsed. A 160 lb man reaches about 0.085% after 3 drinks and a 160 lb woman about 0.105%.

How to read the chart

Each value is the highest estimated BAC from the Widmark formula with no elimination, assuming 14 g of alcohol per standard drink. Time passing lowers the real figure; faster drinking, food and individual differences change it.

Key points

  • At the same weight women reach a higher BAC from the same drinks.
  • Larger or stronger drinks count as more than one standard drink.
  • The US driving limit is 0.08% (0.05% in Utah), but impairment starts below it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a BAC chart?

A table of estimated blood alcohol concentration by body weight, sex and number of drinks.

How accurate is a BAC chart?

It is a population-average estimate. Food, speed of drinking, medications and metabolism change real BAC.

Why is BAC higher for women?

The Widmark factor is 0.55 for women against 0.68 for men, reflecting a lower average share of body water.

Can I use a chart to decide if I can drive?

No. Impairment begins below the legal limit. Use a sober ride.
