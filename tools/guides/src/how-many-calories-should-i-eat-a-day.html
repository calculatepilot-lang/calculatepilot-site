How Many Calories Should I Eat a Day?

A chart of estimated calorie needs by age, sex and activity, and the quick way to turn it into your own daily number.

→ Use the free Calorie Calculator

Short answer: most adults need about 1,600 to 3,000 calories a day. Moderately active women typically need about 1,800 to 2,200 and moderately active men about 2,200 to 2,800, depending on age. For a personal number, estimate your resting calories and multiply by your activity level, which is what the Calorie Calculator does.

These figures assume an average-size adult. A taller, heavier or more muscular person needs more, and a smaller person needs less, which is why a personal calculation beats any table.

How to work out your own number

Calories per day = BMR × activity multiplier
BMR (men) = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 · BMR (women) = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age − 161
  1. A 35-year-old man, 80 kg, 178 cm, exercising 1 to 3 days a week.
  2. BMR = 10 × 80 + 6.25 × 178 − 5 × 35 + 5 = about 1,743 calories.
  3. Maintenance = 1,743 × 1.375 = about 2,396 calories a day.

For a woman, 70 kg, 165 cm and 40 who exercises 3 to 5 days a week, the same steps give about 2,124. The complete method is in how to calculate your TDEE, and the TDEE Calculator shows your number at every activity level.

How low is too low?

The AHA/ACC/TOS obesity guideline suggests roughly 1,200 to 1,500 calories a day for women and 1,500 to 1,800 for men on a weight-loss plan. Very low-calorie diets should be supervised by a healthcare professional. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, needs are higher and different, so use the Pregnancy Calorie Needs Calculator.

Five quick habits that make calorie targets easier

  • Track for two weeks, not forever. It teaches portion sizes and gives you a real baseline.
  • Put protein first at each meal. It helps you feel full. See how much protein you need per day.
  • Cut liquid calories first. Sugary drinks and alcohol add up fast without filling you up.
  • Walk more. Everyday steps raise your maintenance. Try the Steps to Calories Calculator.
  • Weigh yourself on a schedule. Compare weekly averages and adjust by small amounts.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories should I eat a day?

Most adults need roughly 1,600 to 3,000 calories a day. The Dietary Guidelines for Americans estimate about 1,800 to 2,200 for moderately active women and 2,200 to 2,800 for moderately active men, depending on age.

How many calories should a woman eat a day?

Estimated needs for adult women range from about 1,600 calories for sedentary women over 50 to about 2,400 for active women aged 19 to 30. Your own number depends on your size, age and activity.

How many calories should a man eat a day?

Estimated needs for adult men range from about 2,000 calories for sedentary men over 50 to about 3,000 for active men aged 19 to 30.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Subtract a modest deficit from your maintenance calories. A common planning figure is about 500 calories a day for roughly 1 pound a week, but the right size depends on you and a lower-than-necessary intake is not a good idea.

Is 1,200 calories a day enough?

Guidelines such as the AHA/ACC/TOS report suggest roughly 1,200 to 1,500 calories a day for women and 1,500 to 1,800 for men on a weight-loss plan, and very low intakes should be supervised by a healthcare professional.

Why do calorie needs change with age?

Muscle mass and daily activity tend to fall with age, which lowers energy needs. Staying active and keeping strength can slow that change.
