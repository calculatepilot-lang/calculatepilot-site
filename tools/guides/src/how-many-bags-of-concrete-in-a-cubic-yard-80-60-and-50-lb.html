How Many Bags of Concrete in a Cubic Yard? 80, 60 and 50 lb Bags

How many premix bags make a cubic yard, and how to convert any pour into bags.

→ Use the free Concrete Calculator

Short answer: One cubic yard is 27 ft³. An 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 ft³, so it takes 45 bags. A 60 lb bag needs 60 and a 50 lb bag needs 72.

The formula

Bags = volume in ft³ ÷ yield per bag, rounded up. Check the yield printed on your bag, because brands differ slightly.

When bags stop making sense

One cubic yard of 80 lb bags is 45 bags and about 3,600 lb of dry mix to lift and mix. Past about a yard, most people order ready-mix.
