Calories in Mixed Drinks: How Mixers Add Up

Why a cocktail can have far more calories than the spirit in it, and how to estimate yours.

→ Use the free Alcohol Calories Calculator

Short answer: A spirit mixed with regular soda comes to about 178 kcal in this calculator's preset: 98 from alcohol and 80 from 20 g of sugar. Mixers, not the spirit, are usually what push a drink's calories up.

How the calories split

DrinkAlcohol kcalSugar kcalTotal
1.5 oz spirit, neat98098
Spirit + regular soda (7.5 oz total, 8%)9880178

The soda preset assumes 20 g of sugar. Soda, juice, syrup and cream add calories; diet mixers and soda water add almost none.

How to estimate any cocktail

  • Find the ABV and volume of the finished drink (or the spirit pour).
  • Add up the carbs in the mixers and syrups from their labels.
  • Choose Custom in the Alcohol Calories Calculator and enter size, ABV and carbs.

Ways to cut the total

  • Use soda water or a diet mixer in place of regular soda.
  • Ask for a single pour rather than a double.
  • Measure at home: a 1.5 oz pour is one standard drink of spirits.

For beer and wine see the per-serving chart.

Frequently asked questions

Why do cocktails have so many calories?

Sugary mixers, syrups and cream add carb calories on top of the roughly 98 kcal from each standard drink of alcohol.

How many calories are in vodka and soda?

With soda water, about 98 kcal for a 1.5 oz shot of 40% spirit. With regular soda add the sugar calories.

Do diet mixers have calories?

Almost none. The calories then come from the alcohol alone.

How do I count a cocktail's carbs?

Add the carbs from each mixer or syrup on its label, then enter the total under Custom.
