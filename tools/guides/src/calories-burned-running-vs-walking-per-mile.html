Calories Burned Running vs Walking Per Mile

Why running burns only a little more per mile than walking, but far more per minute.

→ Use the free Calories Burned Calculator

Short answer: A 75 kg person burns about 122 calories per mile running at 6 mph and about 100 walking at 3 mph. Running burns far more per minute, but a mile costs only slightly more.

These are gross calories for a 75 kg (165 lb) person using the MET × 3.5 × kg ÷ 200 formula.

What this means

  • If you have 30 minutes, running burns about 2.4 times as much as walking (366 against 150).
  • If you have a mile to cover, the difference is about 20 to 35 calories.
  • Walking is easier on the joints and is easier to keep up daily.

Gross or net?

Gross includes the calories you would burn sitting still during that time. Net removes them. For a mile at 6 mph, the net figure is 109 calories. Net is the better measure of the extra energy the exercise used.

Frequently asked questions

Does running burn more calories than walking per mile?

Slightly. A 75 kg person burns about 122 calories per mile running at 6 mph and about 100 walking at 3 mph.

Which burns more in 30 minutes, walking or running?

Running. At 6 mph it burns about 366 calories in 30 minutes for a 75 kg person, against about 150 for walking at 3 mph.

Do slower runners burn fewer calories per mile?

Per mile, a slower run can cost slightly more, because the MET difference is smaller than the extra time. At 5.0 mph a 75 kg person burns about 134 per mile.

What is the difference between gross and net calories?

Gross includes resting calories during the activity. Net subtracts them using (MET − 1).
