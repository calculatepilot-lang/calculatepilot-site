Activity Level Multipliers: Which One to Choose for Calorie Needs

What the five activity levels mean in a calorie calculator, how much they change your result, and how to avoid picking too high.

→ Use the free Calorie Calculator

Short answer: Calorie calculators multiply your BMR by a factor from 1.2 (sedentary) to 1.9 (extremely active). Choosing one level too high adds around 300 calories to the result.

The five levels

Activity levelMultiplierTypical week
Sedentary1.2Desk job, little or no exercise
Light activity1.375Exercise 1–3 days a week
Moderate activity1.55Exercise 3–5 days a week
Very active1.725Hard exercise 6–7 days a week
Extremely active1.9Physical job plus training

How much the choice changes the answer

Maintenance calories at each level for two example people. The man is 35, 80 kg and 178 cm with a BMR of about 1,743. The woman is 35, 65 kg and 165 cm with a BMR of about 1,345.

Activity levelManWoman
Sedentary (1.2)2,0911,614
Light (1.375)2,3961,850
Moderate (1.55)2,7012,085
Very active (1.725)3,0062,321
Extremely active (1.9)3,3112,556

Each step up adds about 300 calories for the man and about 235 for the woman. From sedentary to extremely active the man's estimate rises by 1,220 calories.

How to choose

  • Count only regular exercise, and pick the level for the average week, not your best week.
  • If you have a desk job and train three times a week, light or moderate activity is usually closer than very active.
  • If you are between two levels, choose the lower one.
  • Your daily steps and movement outside the gym matter too, so a sedentary job with a long walking commute is not quite sedentary.

Checking your choice against real life

Eat at your estimated maintenance for two to three weeks and track your weekly average weight. If it climbs, your real maintenance is lower than estimated. If it falls, it is higher. Move one level, or adjust by 100 to 200 calories, and test again.

Frequently asked questions

What is the activity multiplier in a calorie calculator?

It is the number that your BMR is multiplied by to account for daily movement and exercise, from 1.2 for sedentary up to 1.9 for extremely active.

Which activity level should I pick if I exercise 3 times a week?

Light activity (1.375) or moderate activity (1.55) is usually right. Choose light if the sessions are short or easy, and moderate if they are hard.

What happens if I pick the wrong activity level?

Each level changes the result by roughly 235 to 300 calories. Picking too high makes your target too high, which can stall weight loss.

Can my activity level change over time?

Yes. Recalculate when your training, job or daily steps change.
