How to Measure Your Wrist and Find Your Body Frame Size

A step-by-step way to measure your wrist and work out whether you have a small, medium or large frame.

→ Use the free Body Frame Size Calculator

Short answer: Measure your wrist just below the wrist bone, divide height by wrist (same unit), and compare: men small above 10.4, medium 9.6–10.4, large below 9.6; women small above 11.0, medium 10.1–11.0, large below 10.1.

What you need

  • A flexible tape measure (a strip of paper and a ruler also works).
  • Your height, in the same unit you will use for your wrist.

Step 1: Measure your wrist

Wrap the tape around your wrist at its narrowest point, just below the wrist bone, roughly where a watch strap sits. Keep it level and snug against the skin without squeezing. Measure the same wrist each time.

Step 2: Divide height by wrist

Use the same unit for both numbers. A woman 165 cm tall with a 15.9 cm wrist has a ratio of 165 ÷ 15.9 = 10.4.

Step 3: Compare with the cut-offs

SexSmallMediumLarge
Menabove 10.49.6–10.4below 9.6
Womenabove 11.010.1–11.0below 10.1

A higher ratio means a slimmer wrist for your height. In the example above, 10.4 is a medium frame for a woman.

Step 4: Cross-check with the wrist chart

MedlinePlus publishes a second method: a wrist chart by height band. Our calculator shows both and tells you if they agree. If they land in neighbouring sizes, you are near a border and either label is reasonable.

Common mistakes

  • Measuring over the wrist bone instead of just below it.
  • Mixing units, such as height in centimetres and wrist in inches.
  • Pulling the tape so tight it dents the skin.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I measure my wrist for frame size?

At the narrowest point of the wrist, just below the wrist bone, where a watch strap sits.

Which wrist should I measure?

Either one, as long as you use the same wrist each time. Many guides suggest the dominant hand for consistency.

Do I need to use centimetres?

No. Use any unit as long as height and wrist are in the same unit. The calculator handles both.

Is a ratio of 10.4 small or medium?

For men, 10.4 is the top of the medium range. For women it is medium, because their small frame starts above 11.0.
