eBay Fees Calculator: Final Value Fee Examples

How eBay's final value fee works for most US categories, with worked examples at six sale sizes.

→ Use the free Commission Calculator

Short answer: For most US categories eBay lists a final value fee of 13.6% of the total sale up to $7,500 and 2.35% above, plus $0.30 per order of $10 or less or $0.40 above. A $100 sale costs $14.00.

The fee formula

  • 13.6% of the total amount of the sale, up to $7,500
  • 2.35% of the portion above $7,500
  • Per-order fee: $0.30 for orders of $10 or less, $0.40 for orders over $10

These are the rates on eBay's selling fees page for most US categories on 18 October 2026. Some categories, store subscriptions and extra services have different fees, so check the current page.

Worked example: $10,000 sale

13.6% × 7,500 = $1,020, plus 2.35% × 2,500 = $58.75, plus the $0.40 order fee, for a total of $1,079.15.

Frequently asked questions

What is eBay's final value fee?

For most US categories, 13.6% of the sale up to $7,500 and 2.35% above, plus a per-order fee.

What is the per-order fee?

$0.30 for orders of $10 or less and $0.40 for orders over $10.

Do fees apply to postage?

The fee applies to the total amount of the sale as eBay defines it. Check the fees page for how postage and tax are treated.

Do all categories have the same fee?

No. Some categories and services have different rates.
