MET Values Chart for Common Activities

A chart of MET values for common exercises, how to read them, and how to turn a MET into calories.

→ Use the free Calories Burned Calculator

Short answer: One MET is the energy you use sitting at rest. Activities of 3 to 5.9 METs count as moderate intensity and 6 or more as vigorous. Calories per minute = MET × 3.5 × kg ÷ 200.

How to read a MET value

A MET (metabolic equivalent of task) compares an activity's energy cost with resting. Light activity is under 3 METs, moderate is 3 to 5.9, and vigorous is 6 or more. Calories burned per minute = MET × 3.5 × weight in kg ÷ 200.

Using the chart

To estimate other weights, scale the last column. A 60 kg person burns 80% of the 75 kg figure, and a 90 kg person burns 120%. For a different duration, scale by minutes.

Where the values come from

MET values come from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities. They are averages from studies of many people, so your own burn will vary with fitness, technique, terrain and body composition.

Frequently asked questions

What is a MET?

A MET is a unit that compares the energy cost of an activity with sitting at rest. 1 MET is resting.

What MET counts as moderate or vigorous?

Under 3 is light, 3 to 5.9 is moderate and 6 or more is vigorous.

How do I convert METs to calories?

Calories per minute = MET × 3.5 × weight in kg ÷ 200. Multiply by minutes for a session total.

Are MET values accurate for me?

They are population averages. Your own burn can differ by 10 to 20 percent.
