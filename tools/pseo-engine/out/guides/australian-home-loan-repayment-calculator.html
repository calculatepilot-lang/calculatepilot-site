Quick answer

Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Mortgage Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Loan amount $300,000 at 4.75% a year over 15 years = 180 monthly payments.
  2. Monthly payment = loan x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), where r is the annual rate / 12 (with a 0% rate it is simply loan / n).
  3. Monthly payment = $2,333.50.
  4. Total paid = $2,333.50 x 180 = $420,029.24, of which $120,029.24 is interest.
