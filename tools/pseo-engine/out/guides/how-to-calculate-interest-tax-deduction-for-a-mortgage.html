How to Calculate Interest Tax Deduction for a Mortgage

Quick answer

With these example inputs (loan amount $300,000; interest rate 7.25%; term in years 15 years), the monthly payment (principal and interest) comes to $2,738.59.

Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Mortgage Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Loan amount $300,000 at 7.25% a year over 15 years = 180 monthly payments.
  2. Monthly payment = loan x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), where r is the annual rate / 12 (with a 0% rate it is simply loan / n).
  3. Monthly payment = $2,738.59.
  4. Total paid = $2,738.59 x 180 = $492,945.96, of which $192,945.96 is interest.
