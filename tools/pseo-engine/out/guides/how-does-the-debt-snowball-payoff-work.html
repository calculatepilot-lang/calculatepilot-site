Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Debt Payoff Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Paying only the minimum of $600 clears $9,000 in 19 months with $2,259.91 interest.
  2. Paying $800 a month clears it in 14 months with $1,596.85 interest.
