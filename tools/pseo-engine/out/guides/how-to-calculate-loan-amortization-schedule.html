Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Amortization Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Each month, interest = balance x (5.5% / 12); the rest of the payment reduces the balance.
  2. Scheduled payment = $1,135.58 on $200,000 over 30 years.
  3. Total interest over the full term = $208,808.08.
