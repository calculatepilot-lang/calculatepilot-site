How to Calculate Gross Rental Yield

By Suhaib Hassan · 2026-11-09

How to Calculate Gross Rental Yield: a worked example with the formula shown step by step, a scenario table and the assumptions behind it.

Quick answer

With these example inputs (property price $125,000; annual rent $18,000; annual costs $5,200), the gross rental yield (%) comes to 14.4%.

People also search for: how to calculate rental yield; how to calculate rental yield percentage; how to calculate the rental yield.

Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Rental Yield Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Gross yield = annual rent / price = $18,000 / $125,000 = 14.4%.
  2. Net income = $18,000 - $5,200 costs = $12,800.
  3. Net yield = $12,800 / $125,000 = 10.24%.
