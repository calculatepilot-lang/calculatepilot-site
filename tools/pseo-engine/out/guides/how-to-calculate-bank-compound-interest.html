Quick answer

With these example inputs (starting amount $10,000; interest rate 7.5%; years 10 years; compounding periods per year 12), the final amount comes to $21,120.65.

Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Compound Interest Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Final amount = P x (1 + r/m)^(m x t) with P = $10,000, r = 7.5%, m = 12 periods a year, t = 10 years.
  2. Final amount = $21,120.65.
  3. Interest earned = $21,120.65 - $10,000 = $11,120.65.
