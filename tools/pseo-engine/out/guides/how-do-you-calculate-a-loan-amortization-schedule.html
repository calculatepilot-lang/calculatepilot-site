Quick answer

Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Amortization Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Each month, interest = balance x (6% / 12); the rest of the payment reduces the balance.
  2. Scheduled payment = $1,432.86 on $200,000 over 20 years.
  3. Total interest over the full term = $143,886.91.
