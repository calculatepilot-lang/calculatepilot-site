Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Savings Goal Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Goal $10,000 in 5 years from $0 at 3.25% a year, compounded monthly.
  2. Monthly deposit = (goal - start x (1 + r)^n) x r / ((1 + r)^n - 1); with a 0% rate it is (goal - start) / n.
  3. Monthly deposit = $153.72.
