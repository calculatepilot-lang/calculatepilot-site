These steps use the formula behind the Annuity Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Payments of $1,000 at the end of each month for 17 years at 3% a year.
  2. Future value = payment x ((1 + r)^n - 1) / r; present value = payment x (1 - (1 + r)^-n) / r.
