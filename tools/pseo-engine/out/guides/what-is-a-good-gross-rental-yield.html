What Is a Good Gross Rental Yield

By Suhaib Hassan · 2026-11-02

What Is a Good Gross Rental Yield: a worked example with the formula shown step by step, a scenario table and the assumptions behind it.

Quick answer

With these example inputs (property price $200,000; annual rent $18,000; annual costs $4,400), the gross rental yield (%) comes to 9%.

People also search for: What is a good rental yield?; what is a good rental yield percentage; what is considered a good rental yield.

Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Rental Yield Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Gross yield = annual rent / price = $18,000 / $200,000 = 9%.
  2. Net income = $18,000 - $4,400 costs = $13,600.
  3. Net yield = $13,600 / $200,000 = 6.8%.
