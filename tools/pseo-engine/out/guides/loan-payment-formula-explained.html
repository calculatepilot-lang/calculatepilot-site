Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Loan Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Loan amount $10,000 at 8% a year over 36 months.
  2. Monthly payment = loan x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), where r is the annual rate / 12 (with a 0% rate it is loan / n).
  3. Monthly payment = $313.36; total interest = $1,281.09.
