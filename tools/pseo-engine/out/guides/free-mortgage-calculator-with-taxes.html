Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Mortgage Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Loan amount $300,000 at 7.75% a year over 30 years = 360 monthly payments.
  2. Monthly payment = loan x r / (1 - (1 + r)^-n), where r is the annual rate / 12 (with a 0% rate it is simply loan / n).
  3. Monthly payment = $2,149.24.
  4. Total paid = $2,149.24 x 360 = $773,725.23, of which $473,725.23 is interest.
