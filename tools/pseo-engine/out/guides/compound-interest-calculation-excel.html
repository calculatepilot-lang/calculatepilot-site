Worked example

These steps use the formula behind the Compound Interest Calculator, with the example inputs above.

  1. Final amount = P x (1 + r/m)^(m x t) with P = $10,000, r = 5%, m = 12 periods a year, t = 7 years.
  2. Final amount = $14,180.36.
  3. Interest earned = $14,180.36 - $10,000 = $4,180.36.
