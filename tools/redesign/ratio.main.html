    What is a ratio?

    A ratio compares two or more quantities of the same kind, written A : B or A ÷ B. Two ratios are equivalent if they have the same value: 16 : 9 and 1920 : 1080 are the same ratio. A proportion states that two ratios are equal, A : B = C : D.

    How to calculate with ratios

    • Simplify: divide every part by their greatest common divisor (GCD).
    • Missing value: cross-multiply. From A : B = C : D, A × D = B × C, so D = B × C ÷ A.
    • Scale: multiply every part by the same factor.
    • Split a total: add the parts, divide the total by that sum to get the value of one part, then multiply by each part.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the difference between a ratio and a proportion?expand_more

    A ratio compares quantities, such as 16 : 9. A proportion is an equation that says two ratios are equal, such as 16 : 9 = 1920 : 1080. Proportions let you find a missing value by cross-multiplying.

    How does Euclid’s algorithm simplify a ratio?expand_more

    It finds the greatest common divisor by repeatedly replacing the larger number with the remainder of dividing it by the smaller, until the remainder is zero. The last non-zero remainder is the GCD, and dividing both parts by it gives the lowest terms. It never needs to factorise the numbers.

    Can ratios include decimals or negative numbers?expand_more

    Decimals work: the calculator scales them to whole numbers first (1.5 : 2.5 becomes 15 : 25, then 3 : 5). Ratios of physical quantities are normally positive, so this calculator asks for values above zero.

    What does a ratio of 1 : 2 mean as a fraction or percent?expand_more

    As a part-to-part ratio, 1 : 2 means the second part is twice the first. As shares of the whole, the parts are 1 ÷ 3 = 33.3% and 2 ÷ 3 = 66.7%. Always check whether a ratio is part-to-part or part-to-whole.

    How do I scale a recipe using a ratio?expand_more

    Use “Scale ratio” to set the quantity you want for one ingredient (or the total), and the calculator returns the factor and scaled amounts. Multiply every ingredient by that same factor.

    What if one part of the ratio is zero?expand_more

    A ratio with a zero part, such as 5 : 0, is degenerate — it cannot be scaled back or used to find a missing value, and dividing by zero is undefined. This calculator asks for parts greater than zero.
