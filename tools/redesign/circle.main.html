    What is π and why does it appear?

    π (pi) is the ratio of any circle’s circumference to its diameter, C ÷ d ≈ 3.14159. Because all circles are scaled copies of one another, that ratio is the same for every circle. Johann Lambert proved in 1761 that π is irrational, and Ferdinand von Lindemann proved in 1882 that it is transcendental.

    Key formulas

    • Diameter and radius: d = 2r.
    • Circumference: C = 2πr = πd.
    • Area: A = πr² = C² ÷ 4π.
    • Sector: arc length s = rθ and area ½r²θ, with θ in radians (radians = degrees × π ÷ 180).
    • Segment: area ½r²(θ − sin θ); chord c = 2r sin(θ/2); height h = r(1 − cos(θ/2)).

    Finding one value from another

    • Circumference from diameter: C = πd
    • Diameter from circumference: d = C ÷ π
    • Area from diameter: A = πd² ÷ 4
    • Area from circumference only: A = C² ÷ 4π
    • Circumference from area: C = 2√(πA)

    Example: a circle with a circumference of 44 has a diameter of 14.01, a radius of 7.00 and an area of 154.06.

    Equation of a circle

    A circle with centre (h, k) and radius r has the equation (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r². A circle of radius 5 at the origin is x² + y² = 25. A circle centred at (3, −2) with radius 4 is (x − 3)² + (y + 2)² = 16.

    Units

    Lengths such as radius and circumference use the same unit as the input. Area uses that unit squared, for example cm² or ft². Do not mix units, such as a radius in feet and a height in inches.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is circumference divided by diameter always π?expand_more

    Every circle is a scaled copy of every other. Scaling multiplies the circumference and the diameter by the same factor, so their ratio never changes. That constant ratio is π.

    How do I find the radius from the area or circumference?expand_more

    From the area, r = √(A ÷ π). From the circumference, r = C ÷ 2π. This calculator does both: choose what you know in the first tab.

    What is the difference between a sector and a segment?expand_more

    A sector is a “pie slice” bounded by two radii and an arc. A segment is the region between an arc and its chord. The segment area is the sector area minus the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord.

    How do I find a segment’s area without knowing the angle?expand_more

    From the chord c and height h, first find r = c² ÷ (8h) + h ÷ 2, then the central angle θ = 2·atan2(c ÷ 2, r − h), and use the segment formula ½r²(θ − sin θ). The Segment tab does this for you.

    Should I use degrees or radians?expand_more

    The arc-length and sector-area formulas s = rθ and ½r²θ require radians. If your angle is in degrees, multiply by π ÷ 180 first — or just pick “deg” on the Sector tab and the calculator converts for you.

    What is an annulus?expand_more

    An annulus is the ring between two concentric circles of radii R (outer) and r (inner). Its area is π(R² − r²), which can also be written π(R − r)(R + r).

    How do you find the area of a circle?expand_more

    Use A = πr². A circle with radius 5 has an area of 78.54. If you know the diameter, use A = πd² ÷ 4.

    How do you find the circumference from the diameter?expand_more

    Multiply by π. A diameter of 10 gives a circumference of 31.42.

    How do you find the diameter from the circumference?expand_more

    Divide the circumference by π. A circumference of 44 gives a diameter of 14.01.

    How do you find the area of a circle from its circumference only?expand_more

    Use A = C² ÷ 4π. A circumference of 44 gives an area of 154.06.

    How do I calculate the square feet of a circle?expand_more

    Convert the radius to feet and use A = πr². A 10 ft diameter circle has a radius of 5 ft and an area of 78.54 sq ft. If you measure in inches, divide the area in square inches by 144.

    What is the equation of a circle?expand_more

    (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r², where (h, k) is the centre and r is the radius. At the origin it simplifies to x² + y² = r².

    Does using 3.14 for π change the answer much?expand_more

    Only slightly. For radius 10 the area is 314.00 with 3.14 and 314.16 with the full value, a difference of about 0.05%.

    Why does the area quadruple when the radius doubles?expand_more

    Area depends on the radius squared. Radius 5 gives 78.54 and radius 10 gives 314.16, which is four times as much.
