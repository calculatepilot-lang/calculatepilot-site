Formulas

How the results are worked out

GPA

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) ÷ Σ credits

Cumulative GPA

(prior GPA × prior credits + term points) ÷ total credits

Final exam score needed

needed = (target − current × (1 − w)) ÷ w

Here w is the weight of the final as a decimal. Weighted scales add 0.5 for honors and 1.0 for AP or IB courses to a passing grade.

Good to know

Why your school may differ

Some schools cap GPA at 4.0, some give A+ 4.33, and many high schools use their own weighting. Use your transcript key when in doubt.

A required score above 100% means the target cannot be reached without extra credit.

How to calculate GPA

  • Convert each letter grade to grade points using your school's scale.
  • Multiply each course's grade points by its credit hours.
  • Add the results and divide by the total credit hours.
  • To include past terms, weight your earlier GPA by its credits.

To plan for a final, enter your current grade, your target and the weight of the exam. If the score needed is over 100%, talk to your instructor about extra credit or adjust your goal.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my GPA?expand_more

Multiply each course's grade points by its credits, add them up, and divide by total credits. A 4.0 in a 3-credit course and a 3.0 in a 4-credit course give (12 + 12) ÷ 7 = 3.43.

What is the difference between weighted and unweighted GPA?expand_more

Unweighted GPA uses a plain 4.0 scale. Weighted GPA adds points for harder courses, commonly 0.5 for honors and 1.0 for AP or IB, so it can go above 4.0.

How do I find the grade I need on a final?expand_more

Use (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight. With 84% now, a 90% target and a final worth 25%, you need 108%, which means the target is out of reach without extra credit.

Does the tool include past semesters?expand_more

Yes. Enter your prior cumulative GPA and credits and the tool blends them with the current term to give a new cumulative GPA.

What GPA do I need for the Dean's List?expand_more

It varies by school, but a semester GPA of 3.5 or higher is a common requirement. Check your college's published standard.
