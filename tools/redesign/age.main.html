Method

How exact age is worked out

Years, months, days

subtract year, month and day; if the day is negative, borrow the days of the previous month

Leap year rule

leap if divisible by 4, except centuries not divisible by 400

Total days

days = (end date − birth date) in whole days
Good to know

Leap-day birthdays

People born on 29 February celebrate on 28 February or 1 March in other years, depending on local law. This tool shows 1 March for the next birthday in a non-leap year.

Milestone days count from the date of birth, so your 10,000th day is 10,000 days after you were born.

How to calculate your age

  • Subtract your birth year from the current year to get a first estimate.
  • If your birthday has not happened yet this year, subtract one year.
  • For months and days, count from your last birthday to today using real month lengths.
  • For total days, count the days between the two dates. This tool does all of that for you.

Legal age rules differ by country, and some traditions count age differently. For official purposes, check the rules that apply.

Age difference between two people

Subtract the older birth date from the younger one using the same borrowing method. Example: one person born 3 September 1992 and another born 14 May 1995 are 2 years, 8 months and 11 days apart, which is 983 days.

Western age vs traditional Korean age

The international (Western) age used here counts completed years from the birth date. Traditional Korean age gives a newborn age 1 and adds a year every 1 January, so people are one or two years older than their international age. South Korea moved to international age for most legal and administrative purposes from 28 June 2023.

Calculate age in Excel or Google Sheets

Use =DATEDIF(A1, TODAY(), "Y") for completed years, "YM" for the leftover months and "MD" for the leftover days, where A1 holds the birth date.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate exact age?expand_more

Subtract the birth date from the target date year, month and day. If the day difference is negative, borrow the number of days in the previous month and reduce the month count by one. Do the same for months and years.

How does the calculator handle leap years?expand_more

It uses the real calendar, so 29 February is included in leap years and total days are exact. A year is a leap year if divisible by 4, except century years that are not divisible by 400.

What is my 10,000th day?expand_more

It is the date 10,000 days after you were born, which falls at about 27 years and 4 months old. The tool also shows when you reach 20,000 days and one billion seconds.

What does the life progress bar mean?expand_more

It shows your age as a share of an average life expectancy, which you can edit. It is a statistical average for a group, not a forecast for any one person.

Is my date of birth stored?expand_more

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. The share link only puts your dates in the web address you copy.

What is the difference between Western and Korean age?expand_more

Western (international) age counts completed years from the birth date. Traditional Korean age starts at 1 and adds a year every 1 January, so it is one or two years higher. South Korea adopted international age for most legal and administrative uses on 28 June 2023.

How do I find the age difference between two people?expand_more

Subtract the older birth date from the younger one, borrowing days from the previous month when needed. For example, 3 September 1992 and 14 May 1995 differ by 2 years, 8 months and 11 days (983 days).

Why do age calculators sometimes show different days?expand_more

They borrow days from different months when the day count is negative. This calculator borrows the length of the previous month and uses the real calendar, so total days are exact either way.

How old am I if I was born on 29 February?expand_more

In leap years you have a birthday on 29 February. In other years the legal date is 28 February or 1 March depending on local law, and this tool shows 1 March.

How do I calculate age in Excel or Google Sheets?expand_more

Use DATEDIF with the birth date and TODAY(): "Y" gives completed years, "YM" the remaining months and "MD" the remaining days.

How many days are there between 14 May 1995 and 5 February 2025?expand_more

There are 10,860 days, which is 1,551 weeks and 3 days.

How old is someone born on 14 May 1995 on 5 February 2025?expand_more

29 years, 8 months and 22 days. The day difference is negative, so the calculator borrows the 31 days of January.

When is the 10,000th day for someone born on 14 May 1995?expand_more

It falls on 29 September 2022, which is 14 May 1995 plus 10,000 days.
