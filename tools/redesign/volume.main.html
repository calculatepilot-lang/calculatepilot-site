    What is volume?

    Volume measures the space a solid occupies, in cubic units: a 1 m cube holds 1 m³, which is 1,000 litres. Capacity is the same idea applied to containers. Surface area, by contrast, is the total area of the solid’s outside.

    Cavalieri’s principle

    If two solids have the same height and every horizontal slice at the same level has equal area, the solids have equal volume. This is why a slanted cylinder has the same volume as a straight one, and why a cone is exactly one third of the cylinder with the same base and height.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why is a cylinder’s surface area smallest when its height equals its diameter?expand_more

    For a fixed volume V = πr²h, the surface area is 2πr² + 2V ÷ r. Setting its derivative with respect to r to zero gives h = 2r, so a cylinder that uses the least material for its volume is as tall as it is wide.

    What is the difference between volume, capacity and displacement?expand_more

    Volume is the space the solid occupies. Capacity is how much a container can hold, so it uses the internal dimensions. Displacement is the volume of fluid a submerged object pushes aside, which equals the object’s own volume.

    Are US and imperial gallons the same?expand_more

    No. A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 litres, while an imperial (UK) gallon is exactly 4.54609 litres, about 20% larger. The table shows both.

    How do I find the volume of a partly filled horizontal cylinder?expand_more

    The liquid’s cross-section is a circular segment of depth d in a circle of radius r, with area r²·arccos((r − d) ÷ r) − (r − d)√(2rd − d²). Multiply that area by the cylinder’s length. This calculator handles the full solid; use the Circle Calculator’s segment tool for the cross-section.

    How is the ellipsoid’s surface area calculated?expand_more

    Its volume, 4πabc ÷ 3, is exact. Its surface area has no elementary closed form, so the calculator uses Thomsen’s approximation with exponent 1.6075, which is accurate to about 1%.

    Which units should I enter?expand_more

    Use the same length unit for every dimension. The volume is in that unit cubed, and the conversion table shows it in litres, gallons, cubic feet and other units.
