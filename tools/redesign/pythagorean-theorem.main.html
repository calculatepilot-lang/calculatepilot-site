    Special right triangles

    3-4-5 triangle ratio 3 : 4 : 5

    The smallest Pythagorean triple. Builders use it to check that a corner is square: measure 3 along one edge and 4 along the other, and the diagonal must be exactly 5 (or any multiple, such as 9-12-15).

    45°-45°-90° triangle ratio 1 : 1 : √2

    Half of a square cut along its diagonal. The legs are equal and the hypotenuse is the leg multiplied by √2 (about 1.4142).

    30°-60°-90° triangle ratio 1 : √3 : 2

    Half of an equilateral triangle. The hypotenuse is twice the shortest leg and the other leg is the shortest leg times √3.

    Euclid’s formula for triples m > n > 0

    For whole numbers m > n > 0, the numbers a = m² − n², b = 2mn, c = m² + n² always form a triple. If m and n are coprime and of opposite parity, the triple is primitive.

    Distance in 3D spaced = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)² + (z₂ − z₁)²]

    This applies the theorem twice: first across the floor, then up the height.

    What is the Pythagorean theorem?

    In a right triangle, the square of the hypotenuse (the side opposite the right angle) equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides, the legs: a² + b² = c². It is a special case of the Law of Cosines, in which the term −2ab·cos C vanishes because cos 90° = 0.

    How to use it

    • Hypotenuse: c = √(a² + b²).
    • Missing leg: a = √(c² − b²), and the hypotenuse must be the longest side.
    • Angles: tan α = a ÷ b, so α = arctan(a ÷ b) and the other acute angle is 90° − α.
    • Area and radii: area = ½ab; inradius = (a + b − c) ÷ 2; circumradius = c ÷ 2.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why does a² + b² = c² only work for right triangles?expand_more

    The general rule is the Law of Cosines, c² = a² + b² − 2ab·cos C. For a right angle, cos 90° = 0 and the last term disappears. For an acute angle c² is smaller than a² + b²; for an obtuse angle it is larger.

    What is a primitive Pythagorean triple?expand_more

    A set of whole numbers (a, b, c) with a² + b² = c² whose greatest common divisor is 1, such as 3-4-5 or 5-12-13. Multiples like 6-8-10 are still triples but not primitive.

    How do I find the distance between two points in 3D?expand_more

    Square the differences in x, y and z, add them, and take the square root. It is the Pythagorean theorem applied twice, and it also gives the space diagonal of a box with edges Δx, Δy and Δz.

    What does an exact radical answer like 6√2 mean?expand_more

    When the number under the root is not a perfect square, the root is irrational. It can be simplified by pulling out square factors: √72 = √(36 × 2) = 6√2, which equals about 8.4853. The exact form avoids rounding.

    Can the hypotenuse be shorter than a leg?expand_more

    No. The hypotenuse is always the longest side of a right triangle, because c² = a² + b² is larger than either a² or b². When solving for a leg, the hypotenuse you enter must exceed the known leg.

    Does it work for any units?expand_more

    Yes, as long as all sides use the same unit. The result is in that unit; areas are in square units. The unit-equivalent panel converts the answer to other common lengths.
