Simple habits that keep your savings on track.

The "Pay Yourself First" Principle

Automating a transfer to savings right after each payday means you save before you spend, instead of relying on whatever is left at the end of the month — which often turns out to be very little.

Takeaway: schedule an automatic transfer for the day after payday so saving happens without a decision.
trending_up

The Deposit Timing Advantage

Depositing at the start of each month instead of the end gives every contribution an extra month of interest. The effect is small but free.

A flexible tool for any savings goal

Unlike calculators built around a specific purpose (retirement, a house down payment, college), this one works for any savings target — a wedding, a vacation, a new car, a business investment, or simply a general savings milestone. Choose a timeframe to see the monthly amount you need, or switch to a fixed monthly budget to see how long it will take.

The yield field is optional in effect — set it to 0% if your savings are sitting in a non-interest-bearing account, or use your account's APY if it's in a high-yield savings account or similar low-risk vehicle.

How increasing your contribution changes the timeline

Because this calculation compounds monthly, even modest increases to your monthly contribution can meaningfully shorten your timeline, especially for longer-term goals — the pace scenarios above show the trade-off between saving more aggressively now versus a longer, more comfortable timeline.

If reaching your goal feels too slow at your current contribution rate, this calculator makes it concrete exactly how much increasing your monthly amount would speed things up, which can help make a decision to save more feel more motivating than abstract.

Frequently asked questions

What if I don't earn any interest on my savings?expand_more

Set the annual return field to 0% — the calculator will then simply project based on your contributions alone, without any compounding growth added.

What does it mean if the calculator says my goal isn't reachable?expand_more

This appears if your monthly contribution and return rate combined wouldn't reach the goal within 50 years — in that case, consider increasing your monthly contribution or adjusting your goal amount or timeline expectations.
