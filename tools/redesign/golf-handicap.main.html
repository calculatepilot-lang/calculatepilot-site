Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

sports_golf

Score differential

Each round is converted to a differential using the tee's difficulty. 113 is the slope of a course of standard difficulty.

diff = (113 ÷ slope) × (score − course rating − PCC)
functions

Handicap Index

The average of your best differentials (see the table), plus any adjustment, rounded to one decimal.

index = mean(best differentials) + adjustment
flag

Course and Playing Handicap

The index scaled to a course, then multiplied by the allowance for the format.

course = index × (slope ÷ 113) + (rating − par)
playing = course × allowance

How to calculate a golf handicap

  • Enter each round's adjusted gross score, the tee's course rating and slope, and PCC (usually 0).
  • Add rounds until you have up to 20. At least 3 are needed.
  • Read the Handicap Index, the average of your best differentials.
  • Enter the course rating, slope and par to get the Course and Playing Handicap.

This calculator follows the published World Handicap System method but does not apply the soft cap, hard cap or exceptional score reduction. Your official index comes from your national golf association or club.

Frequently asked questions

How is a Handicap Index calculated?expand_more

Each score becomes a differential: 113 divided by the slope, times the score minus the course rating (and PCC). Your index is the average of your best differentials, for example the lowest 8 of your last 20.

What is the difference between Handicap Index and Course Handicap?expand_more

The index is portable and measures ability on a course of standard difficulty. The Course Handicap converts it to the number of strokes you receive on a specific set of tees.

What is PCC?expand_more

Playing Conditions Calculation. It adjusts differentials for a day when conditions made the course play easier or harder than normal. It is usually 0 and is set by the club, from about −1 to +3.

What is adjusted gross score?expand_more

Your gross score with each hole capped at net double bogey, meaning par plus 2 plus any handicap strokes you receive on that hole.

How many scores do I need?expand_more

At least 3. With fewer than 20 the system uses fewer differentials, and applies a small reduction for 3, 4 and 6 scores.
