Child Adult Height Predictor

Estimate how tall a child may be as an adult. It uses the mid-parental (Tanner) formula from the parents' heights, gives a likely range, and compares the child with the typical height for their age.

Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

family_restroom

Mid-parental (Tanner) height

The average of the parents' heights, with 13 cm (5 in) added for a boy or taken off for a girl.

boy: (father + mother + 13) ÷ 2
girl: (father + mother − 13) ÷ 2
trending_up

Growth channel estimate

If a child stays at the same position against the typical height for their age, the adult height scales the same way. A rough guide only.

adult ≈ height now × (median adult ÷ median at this age)
straighten

Likely range

Adult height is usually within about 8.5 cm (3.3 in) of the mid-parental target.

range = target ± 8.5 cm

How to predict a child's adult height

  • Choose sex and units, then enter age and current height.
  • Enter the biological father's and mother's heights.
  • Read the mid-parental target and range.
  • Compare it with the growth channel estimate. Differences are normal.

These are estimates for children growing normally. They cannot account for puberty timing, illness or nutrition. Talk to a paediatrician if you are worried about growth, or if a child is far above or below their growth chart, and rely on their measurements.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is a height prediction?expand_more

The mid-parental method is usually within about 8.5 cm (3.3 in) of adult height. It cannot see the timing of puberty, so it is an estimate, not a promise.

How does the mid-parental formula work?expand_more

Add the parents' heights, add 13 cm for a boy or subtract 13 cm for a girl, and divide by two. The 13 cm is the average difference between adult men and women.

Does the child's current height change the estimate?expand_more

Not in the mid-parental method. The growth channel estimate does use it, by comparing the child's height with the typical height at their age.

When do children stop growing?expand_more

Girls usually stop growing around 14 to 16, and boys around 16 to 18, though this varies. Growth plates close after puberty.

What if my child is much taller or shorter than predicted?expand_more

Many healthy children are. If a child's growth crosses growth-chart lines or you are concerned, a paediatrician can assess it, sometimes with a bone age X-ray.
