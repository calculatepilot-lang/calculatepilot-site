Formulas

How the numbers are worked out.

straighten

Convert through metres

Multiply by the source unit's length in metres, then divide by the target unit's length in metres.

result = value × (metres per source ÷ metres per target)
architecture

Tape measure reading

Turn the length into inches and round to the nearest fraction.

fraction = round(inches × N) ÷ N, N = 8, 16, 32 or 64

Exact definitions

These relationships are exact by definition, not approximations.

1 in = 25.4 mm
1 mi = 1,609.344 m
1 NM = 1,852 m

How to convert lengths

  • Enter the value and choose the units to convert from and to.

  • Check the table for the same length in every other unit.
  • Use decimal places to control precision.

Frequently asked questions

How many millimetres are in an inch?expand_more

Exactly 25.4. This has been the international definition since 1959.

How many feet are in a mile?expand_more

5,280 international feet, which is exactly 1,609.344 metres.

What is a nautical mile?expand_more

Exactly 1,852 metres. It is about one minute of latitude and is used in aviation and at sea.

How do I read a tape measure fraction?expand_more

The result is the whole inches plus the nearest fraction, such as 9 7/16. Choose 1/32 or 1/64 for finer work.

Why does the converter use metres in the middle?expand_more

Converting every unit to metres first and then to the target avoids compounding rounding errors across steps.
