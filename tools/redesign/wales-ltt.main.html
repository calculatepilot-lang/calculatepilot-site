Worked examples

  • Main residence at £280,000 (GOV.WALES example): 6% on £55,000 = £3,300.
  • Main residence at £300,000: 6% on £75,000 = £4,500.
  • Additional property at £300,000: 5% on £180,000 (£9,000) + 8.5% on £70,000 (£5,950) + 10% on £50,000 (£5,000) = £19,950 — £15,450 more than the main table.
  • Non-residential premises at £300,000: 1% on £25,000 (£250) + 5% on £50,000 (£2,500) = £2,750.

The non-residential nil-rate band of £150,000 applied only from 1 April 2018 to 21 December 2020. It is not the current rule. From 22 December 2020 a non-residential purchase at £150,000 is £0 because the nil band now runs to £225,000, and a £300,000 premises is still £2,750. Do not reuse the old £150,000 threshold.

Worked example with the calculator defaults

The calculator opens on a £285,000 main residence. The main residential bands are 0% to £225,000, 6% to £400,000, 7.5% to £750,000, 10% to £1.5 million and 12% above.

  • 0% on the first £225,000 = £0.
  • 6% on the remaining £60,000 (£225,000 to £285,000) = £3,600.
  • Total LTT: £3,600.

Key takeaways

  • Wales does not charge stamp duty under that name. It charges Land Transaction Tax (LTT), collected by the Welsh Revenue Authority, not English SDLT.
  • The 2026 figures here use the main residential bands (we verified them on 22 Sep 2026). The nil-rate band is £225,000, and there is no separate first-time buyer relief.
  • Additional properties use a separate higher residential table from 11 December 2024. It starts at 5%, not a flat surcharge.
  • The non-residential nil band is £225,000. The old £150,000 band is historical and is not used. The Welsh Revenue Authority, not this calculator, is the authority.

Frequently asked questions

Is there first-time buyer relief on LTT in Wales?expand_more

No. The £225,000 nil-rate band is the standard main-rate threshold for everyone buying a main residence, not a dedicated first-time buyer relief.

How do higher rates work in Wales?expand_more

Since 11 December 2024 additional properties use a separate table starting at 5% on the first £180,000 — not a flat surcharge on the main rates.

Is LTT the same as stamp duty in England?expand_more

No. Different statute, different bands. For England and Northern Ireland use the UK Stamp Duty Calculator.

When do I have to pay LTT?expand_more

The return must be filed and the tax paid to the Welsh Revenue Authority within 30 days of the day after completion — your solicitor normally does this for you.

Does this cover a shop or office?expand_more

Yes — choose Non-Residential. From 22 December 2020 the slices are 0% to £225,000, 1% to £250,000, 5% to £1,000,000 and 6% above. A £300,000 premises is £2,750. The pre-22 December 2020 £150,000 nil band is historical and is not used. Lease rent is not calculated.

Is this a Welsh Revenue Authority assessment?expand_more

No. Confirm with a solicitor. This page is a planning estimate from published bands.

Does Wales have stamp duty?expand_more

Not under that name. Wales charges Land Transaction Tax (LTT), collected by the Welsh Revenue Authority. England and Northern Ireland use Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT).

How much LTT do I pay on £300,000 in Wales?expand_more

A main residence pays £4,500. An additional property pays £19,950 on the higher residential rates. Non-residential premises pay £2,750.

What are the Welsh LTT residential rates?expand_more

Main residential: 0% to £225,000, 6% to £400,000, 7.5% to £750,000, 10% to £1.5 million and 12% above. Additional properties use the higher residential table, which starts at 5%.
