## RSA Structure and Rules

**Per RSA ad:**
- Up to 15 headlines (30 characters each)
- Up to 4 descriptions (90 characters each)
- Google dynamically combines headlines and descriptions based on search context
- Aim for "Good" or "Excellent" ad strength

**Character limits:**
| Element | Max Characters | Display |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| Headline | 30 | Up to 3 headlines shown |
| Description | 90 | Up to 2 descriptions shown |
| Display URL path | 15 per field (2 fields) | domain.com/path1/path2 |

**Rules for effective RSAs:**
1. Write 15 unique headlines — don't repeat the same message with slight variations
2. Each headline should make sense on its own AND in combination with any other headline
3. Don't rely on a specific headline order (Google rotates) — unless you pin
4. Include keywords naturally but don't keyword-stuff
5. At least 3 headlines should mention a CTA
6. At least 2 headlines should include your primary value proposition
7. Diverse angles maximize Google's ability to find winning combinations

## Headline Framework by Funnel Stage

### Top of Funnel (TOFU) — Awareness / Educational

Goal: Introduce the category or problem. Attract broad interest.

| Angle | Template | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Problem awareness | "{Problem}? There's a Better Way" | "Spending Hours on Payroll?" |
| Discovery | "Discover How {Outcome}" | "Discover How Teams Save 10 Hrs/Wk" |
| Question | "Still {Pain Point}?" | "Still Managing HR in Spreadsheets?" |
| Category | "The Modern {Product Category}" | "The Modern HR Platform" |
| Education | "What {Audience} Need to Know" | "What Growing Teams Need to Know" |

### Middle of Funnel (MOFU) — Consideration / Comparison

Goal: Build credibility, differentiate from alternatives.

| Angle | Template | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Social proof | "Trusted by {Number}+ {Audience}" | "Trusted by 2,000+ HR Teams" |
| Rating/award | "Rated #{X} {Category}" | "Rated #1 HR Software for SMBs" |
| Comparison | "The {Competitor} Alternative" | "The BambooHR Alternative" |
| Proof point | "{Specific Result} Guaranteed" | "60% Less Admin Time Guaranteed" |
| Authority | "Used by {Notable Company/Type}" | "Used by Fortune 500 HR Departments" |

### Bottom of Funnel (BOFU) — Decision / Action

Goal: Drive conversion. Urgency, offers, clear CTAs.

| Angle | Template | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Offer | "Get {X}% Off {Product}" | "Get 20% Off Your First Year" |
| CTA - demo | "Book a {Timeframe} Demo" | "Book a 15-Minute Demo" |
| CTA - trial | "Start Your Free {Product} Trial" | "Start Your Free Trial Today" |
| Urgency | "{Offer} — Ends {Date}" | "Holiday Pricing — Ends Dec 31" |
| Risk reversal | "{Guarantee} or {Outcome}" | "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee" |
| Easy start | "{Product} in {Timeframe}" | "Set Up in Under 2 Weeks" |

## Headline Angle Categories

For each RSA, aim to cover at least 8 of these 12 angles across your 15 headlines:

| # | Angle | Purpose | Example |
|---|-------|---------|---------|
| 1 | **Primary value prop** | What you do and why it matters | "All-in-One HR Management Platform" |
| 2 | **Benefit-driven** | Outcome the customer gets | "Save 10+ Hours Per Week on HR" |
| 3 | **Feature-specific** | Key feature or capability | "Onboarding, Payroll & Compliance" |
| 4 | **Social proof** | Trust through numbers or names | "Trusted by 2,000+ Companies" |
| 5 | **CTA - primary** | Main desired action | "Get a Free Demo Today" |
| 6 | **CTA - secondary** | Alternative action | "See Pricing & Plans" |
| 7 | **Objection handling** | Address top concern | "No Long-Term Contracts" |
| 8 | **Audience-specific** | Call out the target user | "Built for Growing HR Teams" |
| 9 | **Differentiator** | What makes you unique | "The Only HR Tool with Built-In Payroll" |
| 10 | **Authority/trust** | Credibility indicators | "SOC 2 Certified & GDPR Compliant" |
| 11 | **Urgency/scarcity** | Time-sensitive motivation | "Limited Time: 20% Off Annual Plans" |
| 12 | **Pricing/value** | Cost-related messaging | "Plans Starting at $5/Employee/Mo" |

## Description Frameworks

Descriptions have 90 characters — use them for complete thoughts that expand on headlines.

**Framework 1: Feature-Benefit-CTA**
"[Feature] to [benefit]. [CTA] today."
→ "Automate onboarding, payroll, and time tracking to save 10+ hrs/wk. Start your free demo today."

**Framework 2: Proof-Differentiator-CTA**
"[Social proof]. [Key differentiator]. [CTA]."
→ "Join 2,000+ HR teams. The only platform with built-in payroll + compliance. Book a demo now."

**Framework 3: Problem-Solution-CTA**
"[Pain point]? [Your solution]. [CTA]."
→ "Drowning in HR admin? [Brand] automates it all in one place. See a demo in 15 minutes."

**Framework 4: Audience-Benefit-Trust**
"[For audience], [benefit]. [Trust signal]."
→ "For growing teams of 10-500, cut HR admin time by 60%. SOC 2 compliant, setup in days."

## Pin Strategy

Pinning locks a headline to a specific position. Use sparingly — over-pinning limits Google's optimization.

**When to pin:**
| Position | Pin When | Example |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Position 1 | Brand name or primary value prop must always show | "All-in-One HR Software" |
| Position 2 | CTA must always be visible | "Book Your Free Demo" |
| Position 3 | Rarely pin — let Google optimize | — |

**Pin rules:**
- Pin maximum 2 positions (ideally just 1)
- Pin 2-3 headline OPTIONS to each pinned position (Google still has variety)
- Never pin all 15 headlines — defeats the purpose of RSAs
- If you pin only 1 headline to position 1, ad strength will drop — pin 2-3 alternatives

## Testing and Iteration

**Using 2025 asset-level reporting:**
1. Review individual headline and description performance ratings (Low, Good, Best)
2. Replace "Low" rated assets with new variations
3. Keep "Best" rated assets — they're your proven winners
4. Test new angles in the "middle" — replacing "Good" rated assets lets you experiment without losing top performers

**Testing cadence:**
- Review asset performance monthly
- Replace 2-3 underperforming headlines per RSA per month
- Keep at least 5 proven headlines stable for continuity
- Test one new messaging angle at a time for clear signal

**What to test:**
- Different value props (speed vs cost vs quality)
- Emotional vs rational appeals
- Specific numbers vs general claims
- Different CTAs (demo vs trial vs contact vs quote)
- Including price vs not including price

**Ad copy n-gram analysis:** Frederick Vallaeys' approach — analyze which words/phrases in your ad copy correlate with higher CTR and conversion rates. Use the **google-ads-scripts** skill for automated ad copy analysis scripts.
